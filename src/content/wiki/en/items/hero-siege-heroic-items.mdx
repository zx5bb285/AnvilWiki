## The Tier Between Two Games

Hero Siege has two economies that meet in the middle of its [item ladder](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/): the leveling game, where any decent affix clears the next pack, and the endgame game, where every slot is a plan. Heroic items are where the two economies shake hands — the first tier whose drops look like endgame gear while remaining common enough to find during a normal season's climb.

<Callout type="info" title="Tier positions are community-mapped">
Hero Siege publishes no official rarity hierarchy. The band structure described here — Heroic below [Satanic](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/), Satanic below the [Angelic](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) and [Unholy](/items/hero-siege-unholy-items/) peak — reflects guide consensus and current player practice; verify roll quality in-game.
</Callout>

## What Heroic Quality Means in Practice

A Heroic drop is the game telling you your build has a future. The affixes are endgame-relevant rather than leveling-filler, the item is worth equipping instead of vendoring, and the slot it lands in usually stays filled for a meaningful stretch of the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/).

The tier's practical role is momentum. Players who stall mid-season are almost always wearing a mixed bag of leveling drops; players who transition smoothly into [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) and [Uber](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) content usually did it on a Heroic spine — good-enough pieces in every slot, farmed or [bought](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) cheaply, holding the line while the top band takes its time.

## Where Heroic Fits the S10 Gear Arc

Read from the top down, Hero Siege's endgame arc has four phases, and Heroic owns the second:

| Phase | What fills the slots | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Leveling | Anything that clears the act | [Campaign](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/) drops |
| **Transition** | **Heroic pieces** | Late-campaign and upper-middle ladder farming |
| Endgame core | [Satanic](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) spine | Wormholes, Ubers, Inferno farming |
| Peak | [Angelic](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) / Unholy upgrades | Deep endgame volume, [Codex crafting](/guides/hero-siege-codex-guide/), market |

The table's real lesson is the arrows: nothing in the transition phase is permanent, and treating Heroic pieces as temporary on purpose is what makes the phase fast. [Season 10's systems](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) — the talent and Ether expansions, the reworked market — all assume you are moving, not collecting.

## How to Know a Heroic Slot Is Done

Three signals, roughly in order:

1. **You are farming content above its pay grade.** If your routine is upper-ladder endgame, the [drop pool](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) has already left the tier behind.
2. **The slot is on your build's critical path.** Damage-scaling slots graduate first; utility slots serve longest.
3. **A replacement is within reach** — a farmed upgrade, a [market listing](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) at [gold-farming](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) distance, or a [Codex](/guides/hero-siege-codex-guide/) attempt sequence. Replacing downward is not a transition, it is a detour.

## Heroic FAQ

### Should I upgrade Heroic items or replace them?

For build-critical slots, replacement planning starts the moment you enter endgame farming. For utility slots, upgrading a Heroic piece can be the cheaper move — the community's advice differs by item type, so verify upgrade outcomes in-game before spending.

### Do Heroic items sell on the market?

They trade, at transition-tier prices. The [market guide](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) covers the pricing logic — Heroic pieces are the volume of the market rather than its headlines.

### Is a great-roll Heroic better than a bad Satanic?

Sometimes, yes — roll quality can outrun a tier label in the short term. But the tier bands exist because, on average, higher tiers roll better ranges; treat a lucky Heroic as a bridge, not a destination.
