## The Upgrade You Don't Need

Most build pages open by telling you what to chase. This one opens by telling you what to stop chasing. The [Stormweaver](/guides/hero-siege-stormweaver-build/) already clears packed screens better than nearly any class — chain lightning turns density into damage, so more area power is a want, not a need. What the class actually lacks is boss damage: against a single target, the chains have nothing to arc through and the build's damage falls off a cliff. Every slot decision below is ranked by how much it closes that gap.

<Callout type="warn" title="Structural map, live numbers">
Post-rework casting values move with 7.0.x patches. The single-target premise and slot ordering hold across those changes; confirm specific item rolls in-game before committing gold.
</Callout>

## The Single-Target Gap, Slot by Slot

Rank the board by boss damage, not by clear power:

1. **Single-target and boss-damage modifiers — the whole project.** Affixes that raise damage against a single target lead every farming decision, because they buy the thing the kit does not have.
2. **A single-target skill's damage and skill-level modifiers.** Community guides recommend carrying one non-chaining skill for boss fights; its modifiers outrank generic area affixes on the board.
3. **Cast speed — the tempo band, aimed at bosses.** Faster casting shortens boss phases, which is where the class loses time. It doubles as safety during long single-target exchanges.
4. **Enough chain and area power to stay a chain caster — the floor.** Not a growth stat, a floor. You keep just enough that the clear identity survives.

## What the Clear Side Gives Up

Closing the boss gap is a trade, and the trade is affordable because the class has clear power to spare. The slots a Stormweaver can give up:

- **The area-stacking affixes.** The class does not need to over-invest in area the way a build with poor clear would. Moving those lines to single-target modifiers costs almost nothing in clear speed.
- **An entire "more chains" obsession.** Once the baseline clear is comfortable, extra chain links stop being the priority; the boss lane gets the budget instead.

The one thing that does **not** get cut is the general cast-speed and build engine — it feeds both the boss lane and the clear, so it stays.

## The Boss Lane, Ranked

For the endgame content that punishes single-target weakness hardest:

- **[Uber fights](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/)** are the reason this page exists. Learn each arena's pull spots so you can keep a few adds chained during the encounter and feed the single-target skill honest windows.
- **[Wormhole guardian phases](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/)** are the other bottleneck — density peaks through the floors and then collapses on the guardian, which is exactly where a clear-built Stormweaver stalls without boss-lane gear.
- **[Satanic-tier and up pieces](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/)** are where the boss lane gets filled; the [rarity ladder](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) explains the tiers before you spend.

## When a Stormweaver Slot Is Done

The finish line is measurable, and it is not clear speed. **A Stormweaver slot is done when your boss-fight time drops** — when a guardian or Uber phase that used to drag now closes in fewer passes. A slot that raises your clear speed while your boss time is flat is a slot that solved a problem the class never had.

That single test is what keeps this build honest. It is easy to keep buying area power because the sheet looks better and the screens empty faster; it is correct to stop as soon as boss time stops falling, and put the budget back into the one place the class is actually short.

## Stormweaver BiS FAQ

### Is Area damage a wasted stat on the Stormweaver?

Not wasted, but no longer a growth priority. Keep enough to stay a chain caster; past that floor, single-target modifiers buy more than more area ever will, because the class already has the clear.

### How do I test whether my boss damage is actually improving?

Time the fights, not the screens. Pick one guardian or Uber phase as a benchmark and run it after each upgrade — falling boss time is the only signal that matters for this build.

### Do I need the class's single-target skill geared separately?

It helps. Community guides suggest carrying one non-chaining skill precisely for boss and sparse-arena fights, so affixes that support it earn their slot. Keep the chain kit as the clear tool and let the single-target loadout carry the boss lane.
