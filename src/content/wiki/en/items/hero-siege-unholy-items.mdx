## The Other Top Tier

Hero Siege's endgame item culture has two headline rarities, and [Angelic](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) gets most of the headlines. Unholy is the other name in that top slice — a peer tier at the ceiling of the [item system](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/), not a step below it. When an endgame player lists the pieces that define a finished build, both names appear in the same breath.

Understanding Unholy starts with dropping the assumption that one purple-and-gold color code is "the best." At the top of this ladder, tiers describe families of power, and the practical question is always what a specific piece does for a specific build.

<Callout type="info" title="Verified by practice, not by patch notes">
Hero Siege publishes no official rarity tables, and tier behavior at the top of the ladder is community-documented. Specifics below reflect current endgame practice in Season 10; exact rates and roll ranges deserve in-game verification.
</Callout>

## Where Unholy Sits on the Ladder

The working map of Hero Siege's rarity top slice, as the community draws it: the [Satanic](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/), [Set](/items/hero-siege-set-items/) and [Heroic](/items/hero-siege-heroic-items/) tiers form the upper-middle band, and **Angelic and Unholy form the peak** above them. Within that peak, neither tier is canonically "above" the other — they are the two faces of endgame item power, and guides treat them as a pair.

For a returning player, the practical takeaway is orientation, not ordering: any top-slice item — Angelic or Unholy — is a build-defining event.

## What an Unholy Piece Does for a Build

The reason top-slice items matter so much in Hero Siege is affix economics. Below the peak, every gear slot is a negotiation — good stats wrapped around compromises. An Unholy piece in the right slot ends the negotiation: its affixes are what the build was trying to approximate with everything beneath it.

That is why [BiS lists](/items/hero-siege-marksman-bis/) for classes read the way they do — they are maps of which slots have a peak-tier answer, and Unholy answers appear on those maps as often as Angelic ones.

## Hunting Unholy: Where the Routes Run

Unholy sourcing runs through the same endgame arteries as everything at the power ceiling:

- **Volume endgame farming** — [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pushes and [Uber](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) rotations at the top of the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/). Volume is the mechanic; fast [builds](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) are the multiplier.
- **The Codex loop** — the [crafting system](/guides/hero-siege-codex-guide/) that turns materials into targeted attempts, the community's answer to drop randomness at every top tier.
- **The market** — a peer player's find surfaces on the [Tarethiel Market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) eventually. Peak-tier listings are priced like what they are, but for a build-critical slot, gold is often cheaper than weeks.

## Unholy FAQ

### Are Unholy items uniques?

The top tiers describe rarity quality within the broader [unique and rarity system](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) — what changes at the peak is the power and affix profile of the piece, not the basic logic of how items equip and scale.

### Should I sell or keep a duplicate Unholy?

Check the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) before deciding. Peak-tier duplicates — especially in slots other builds want — are among the most tradable items in the game, and Season 10's reworked market made realizing that value easier.

### How many Unholies does a finished build need?

However many slots have a peak-tier answer for your class — typically a handful, not a full kit. Build guides and [BiS pages](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) map the specific slots per class.
