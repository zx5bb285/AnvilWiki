## BiS as a Roadmap, Not a Shopping List

A warning before the fun part: Hero Siege's endgame item pool is deep enough that any "here are the five exact items" list goes stale with the next balance patch. What stays stable is the structure underneath — which slots the [Whirlwind Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) needs filled first, what each slot's job is, and the routes that fill it. That structure is what this page maps.

<Callout type="info" title="Priority map, patch-safe">
Slot priorities below follow from the build's mechanics and hold across 7.0.x patches. Individual best items change frequently; verify any specific piece against your own in-game rolls before committing.
</Callout>

## Slot Priority: Damage First, Immortality Second

The Whirlwind core defines the order everything else follows:

1. **Weapon — the whole build.** Spin damage scales from the weapon, so this slot climbs the [rarity ladder](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) first: a functional leveling weapon, then a [Heroic](/items/hero-siege-heroic-items/) placeholder, then a [Satanic](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) endgame spine, then a peak-tier [Angelic](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) or [Unholy](/items/hero-siege-unholy-items/) finisher. Every other slot waits.
2. **Durability slots — the Viking tax office.** The build's identity is standing in danger; life, mitigation and regen affixes on armor slots are what let the spin stay switched on inside packs instead of dancing at their edge.
3. **Attack-speed and spin-upkeep affixes — the multiplier band.** More uptime and faster spins convert directly into clear speed without touching the rotation.
4. **Utility last.** Movement quality-of-life and convenience affixes are real but never urgent — the [starter progression](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) fills them by accident.

## Filling the Slots: Three Routes

### Drops — the default engine

The Viking farms the way it fights: face-forward. [Wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pushes, [Uber](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) rotations and high-[difficulty](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) routes produce the tier pressure that upgrades each slot on the ladder's schedule — weapon first, because the weapon's power keeps the farming loop fast enough to feed everything else.

### The market — the gap closer

The [reworked Tarethiel Market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) is where a stuck slot becomes a solved one. The Viking's BiS path uses it for two moments: buying the mid-tier weapon that unblocks endgame entry, and closing single armor slots where farming RNG has stalled. Both are [gold-income](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) problems, not luck problems.

### The Codex — the weapon project

The weapon slot is exactly what the [Codex of Eternity](/guides/hero-siege-codex-guide/) exists for: targeted crafting attempts at the damage affixes the spin scales with, converting farmed materials into attempts instead of waiting for the right base to drop.

## When a Viking Slot Is "Done"

- **Weapon:** when upgrades stop changing your clear speed — for most of a season that is a high-tier Satanic piece, with peak-tier as the stretch goal.
- **Durability slots:** when the content you run stops threatening them — if [Inferno](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) packs no longer dent you, the slot is done earning upgrades.
- **Everything else:** when the affix serves the spin and nothing in your farming loop is begging for the slot's budget.

That "done" definition matters more for the Viking than most classes, because the build's durability buys it the luxury of farming indefinitely — the discipline is knowing which slots have already paid off.

## Viking BiS FAQ

### Should I prioritize a second damage stat or more defense?

Through the transition phase, defense wins — a dead Viking farms nothing. Once Inferno content stops threatening you, shift budget toward the multiplier band: attack speed and spin upkeep.

### Do specific unique items change the priority order?

Occasionally a unique power reshapes a slot's value, and patch notes reshuffle individuals constantly. The slot map above survives those changes because it follows from the build's mechanics, not from any one item.

### Does this roadmap work for party play?

Yes, with one shift: in groups the Viking's durability does even more work (holding packs while ranged classes damage them), so the durability band graduates slightly earlier than in solo play.
