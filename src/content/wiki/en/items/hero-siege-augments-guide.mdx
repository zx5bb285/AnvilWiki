## What Are You Actually Buying With an Angelic Key?

A lot of Hero Siege's late-game power never touches a gear slot, and Angelic Augments are the clearest example: upgrades that attach to the equipment you already chose rather than replacing it. The whole system runs on a single currency, and community documentation is consistent about the shape of the cost:

1. **Adding an augment** to a piece of gear costs an Angelic Key.
2. **Upgrading an augment** to its next level costs another Angelic Key.
3. **Every upgrade re-opens the door.** Pushing an augment forward requires re-entering the Angelic Realm, and one entry itself costs a Key — unless you come in through Bifröst, the documented alternative route.

That third line is the one players underestimate. The Key is not just paying for the stat; it is paying for the trip. Anyone planning an augment budget around "one Key per level" is planning around half the bill.

## Why Every Upgrade Is a Field Trip

The design consequence of the re-entry rule is bigger than it looks: augment progress is gated by Angelic Realm runs, not by anything you can stockpile in a stash tab. Gold, dust and runes all bank; entry does not. Two Keys per level — one at the upgrade, one at the door — is the documented pattern, and it means your augment speed is your Angelic Realm visit speed.

The Bifröst exception is worth knowing without being worth over-planning around. Community sources describe it as an alternate way into the Realm that can spare you the entry Key, but the details around it are thin and patch-sensitive. Treat it as "the route that sometimes saves a Key," verify how it behaves in your current patch, and do not build a farming schedule on it.

<Callout type="tip" title="Keys are a currency, so budget them like one">
Angelic Keys do not serve augments alone — endgame unlock chains consume them too. The recipe for Uber [Amun Ra](/bosses/hero-siege-amun-ra-boss/), for instance, includes one Key in its Ankh. Decide which system your next Key belongs to before you start clicking upgrade buttons; the augments will still be there next week, but a locked Uber gate wastes a whole evening.
</Callout>

## How Should You Pace Your Upgrades?

The honest answer is: front-load, then stop on feel. Early augment levels are infrastructure — cheap relative to your whole gear project and applied to items you will wear for a while. Deep upgrade chains are luxury, because the per-level cost never gets cheaper while your Key income is never guaranteed.

Two practical rules fall out of that:

- **Augment items you have already committed to.** Upgrading gear you plan to replace next weekend is two Keys donated to the void. The [rarity ladder](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) decides when an item is worth attaching power to, not the augment menu.
- **What each level actually grants is the least-documented part of the system.** Exact per-level values are community-compiled and patch-sensitive, so verify what your next level buys in-game before deciding it is worth two Keys. This page maps the economy; it will not pretend the numbers are nailed down.

One more lever exists: supply. Keys drop commonly enough on the top difficulties that community guides treat buying one as a routine purchase rather than a trophy, so a stubborn augment plan can be rescued on the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) — usually a cheaper fix than re-rolling the gear underneath it.

## What Happened to Glyphs? Read This Before Any Old Guide

Augments did not appear out of nowhere — they are the survivors of a system the game deleted, and the history matters because old advice is still everywhere. Three eras, in order:

1. **The glyph era.** Glyphs were socketed into Heroic Nodes on the Hero Level Tree and farmed from Challenge Dungeons. If you are reading a guide that says "farm Challenge Dungeons for glyphs," you are reading about this era.
2. **Season 5 — the conversion.** Existing glyphs were converted into augments, and the Angelic Key became the currency for acquiring and upgrading them.
3. **Season 9 — the removal.** Glyphs and Essence of Incarnation were removed outright, in the same season that brought the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) as the new progression layer on that side of the character sheet.

So the plain statement, without diplomatic softening: **any guide telling you to farm glyphs in Season 10 is outdated.** The sockets it describes, the currency it names and the dungeon it sends you to belong to a system that no longer exists. Close the tab and come back to a page that admits what season it is.

## Where Augments Sit in the Wider Item Economy

Augments ride on gear; they do not choose it. The sequence that works for most players is the same one that governs the rest of endgame: settle your [Angelic-tier](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) and top-tier targets first, attach augments to the survivors, and let targeted systems handle the rest. That is also the logic of the [Codex of Eternity](/guides/hero-siege-codex-guide/) — Hero Siege's endgame repeatedly asks you to invest in what you already own rather than gamble on the next drop, and augments are that philosophy in its purest form.

## Angelic Augments FAQ

### Do I really have to re-enter the Angelic Realm for every single level?

That is the documented pattern: each upgrade pairs its Key with a fresh entry, and each entry costs a Key of its own. It is the reason augment progress feels slow even for players with healthy Key income — the trip is the tax, not the stat.

### What is Bifröst, exactly?

Community documentation names it as the alternative route into the Angelic Realm — the way to enter without spending an entry Key. Beyond that, details are sparse and patch-sensitive, so this page deliberately stops where the documentation stops: know the name, test it in-game, and treat any confident explanation of its mechanics with suspicion.

### Should I save my Angelic Keys for Uber unlocks instead of augments?

There is no universal answer, only a priority order you set yourself. Keys spent on an unlock chain open content you can farm every week; Keys spent on augments raise the ceiling of gear you already own. If endgame entry is your bottleneck, unlock first. If your build is capped by its gear ceiling, augments first — and if you are short either way, remember the Key itself is the cheap part.
