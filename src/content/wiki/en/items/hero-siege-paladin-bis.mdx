## One Class, Two Slot Maps

Most class BiS pages can start with a single priority list. The Paladin cannot. Its [build guide](/guides/hero-siege-paladin-build/) frames the class as two paths in one shell — a **Lightning Fury** melee direction and a **Fist of the Heavens** casting direction — and each one wants the same slots filled in a different order. Gearing a Paladin is therefore a two-step job: pick a path, then rank that path's board. This page does both, in that order.

<Callout type="info" title="Choose, then shop">
Slot values below are path-specific by design. If you have not committed yet, build the shared spine first and let your first strong drop or [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) purchase pick the side. Half-committing both paths gears neither.
</Callout>

## The Melee Board: Gear That Serves Lightning Fury

If Lightning Fury is the core, the weapon leads — but it leads for damage and attack speed rather than for elemental casting:

1. **The weapon, as a damage source again.** Melee Paladins are the one place the class buys white damage first. The weapon climbs the [rarity ladder](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) ahead of everything else.
2. **Lightning modifiers — the melee path's key multiplier.** This is where the two boards diverge most. Lightning damage multiplies the path's identity skill, so it outranks generic damage affixes.
3. **Attack speed and area — the clear band.** Faster swings and wider hits convert the weapon into pack damage without touching the rotation.
4. **Durability — the melee tax.** Standing in the fray costs health; a mitigation band keeps the path's aggression sustainable.

## The Caster Board: Gear That Serves Fist of the Heavens

If Fist of the Heavens is the core, the weapon drops out of first place, exactly as it does on the [Pyromancer](/items/hero-siege-pyromancer-bis/):

1. **Holy-damage and skill modifiers — the new engine.** The path's damage is cast, not swung, so these climb first.
2. **Cast speed — tempo and safety.** Faster casts mean more output and less time exposed, which matters because a Paladin caster still fights closer than a pure mage.
3. **The weapon as a modifier carrier.** Same switch as any caster: shop for the affixes bolted onto it, not for its swing damage.
4. **Durability — a smaller tax here.** The class's base sturdiness already covers more than a Pyromancer's, so the casting path needs less defensive budget than the melee path.

## The Shared Spine Both Paths Agree On

A handful of slots do not care which path you chose, and filling them early is never wasted:

- **Movement and utility affixes.** Both paths want to be where they need to be, and both benefit from the same mobility pieces.
- **A baseline mitigation floor.** The Paladin's class durability serves both boards; the spine just makes it reliable.
- **The [Incarnation Tree's](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) defensive band.** Both paths claim survivability nodes first at level 100, before path-specific offense.

Spine slots are the reason delaying commitment works here. You can gear them before you have decided, then spend the path slots once a drop or purchase makes the choice obvious.

## The Respec Trigger

The cleanest signal for redrawing the board is a **gear-changing session** — a new unique, a strong melee base, or a set of [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) purchases. When your drops start repeating an element (lightning weapons, holy cast pieces), that is the game telling you which board you actually have. Respec after that evidence, never before it.

## When a Paladin Slot Is Done

Use a respec as the test. A slot is finished when **switching paths would not move it** — if it survives the leap from Lightning Fury to Fist of the Heavens, it was a spine slot and it has done its job. A slot that *would* move is path gear, and it is done only when it has fully signed with one path: when every affix on it serves that path's key multiplier and nothing on it hedges the other.

Half-signed slots are the Paladin's real bottleneck. A piece built for a compromise between swinging and casting serves neither, which is why the closing rule for this class is commitment rather than raw power.

## Paladin BiS FAQ

### Can I blend the two paths on one gear set?

In the transition phase, yes — spine slots make a blended set functional. Past early endgame it stops paying: the two paths want opposite things from the weapon slot especially, so a blended set is mediocre at both instead of good at one.

### Does the melee path still want holy or lightning on gear?

Lightning is the melee path's multiplier and belongs on the board. Generic holy affixes matter less when you are swinging; prioritize them only if a piece also carries the lightning or attack-speed you actually need.

### Where do I fill a stalled Paladin slot?

The same three routes as any class: farming for drops, the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) to buy past RNG, and targeted crafting once the path is set. The difference for a Paladin is that you should know *which board* the slot belongs to before you spend on it.
