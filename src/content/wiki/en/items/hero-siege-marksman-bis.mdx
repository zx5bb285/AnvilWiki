## Gearing an Army Instead of a Character

Most Hero Siege [BiS conversations](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) are about sharpening your own attacks. The [drone Marksman](/guides/hero-siege-marksman-build/) is the inversion of that project: the build's damage is automated, which means your gear's job is not to make you hit harder — it is to make your drones hit harder, survive longer and cover more ground. Read every slot priority below through that inversion and the whole list makes sense.

<Callout type="info" title="The patch caveat, as always">
Drone scaling and specific best items move with 7.0.x balance patches, and the community's deep-dive guides document individual breakpoints. The slot map below follows from the build's automation mechanics and survives those shifts; verify specific items in-game.
</Callout>

## Slot Priorities for the Drone Core

1. **Minion-amplification slots — the build's engine.** Whatever amplifies drone output sits at the top of every farming decision. These slots climb the [rarity ladder](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) first, from [Heroic](/items/hero-siege-heroic-items/) placeholders to [Satanic](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) endgame spines to [peak-tier](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) finishers.
2. **Survivability for you, not the drones — the fail-safe.** The automation keeps fighting while you reposition, but you are the single point of failure. Enough life and mitigation to survive mistakes keeps the army on the field.
3. **Attack speed and automation tempo — the multiplier band.** The build scales with how fast its automated damage acts, so tempo affixes convert directly into clear speed.
4. **Personal attack stats — last on purpose.** A drone Marksman's own trigger-pulls are cleanup, not damage. Slots that only buff your own shots fill themselves with whatever drops.

## The Three Gear Routes, in Build Order

### Phase 1 — drops get you to endgame

The drone build's famous accessibility comes from its tolerance for random gear: the automated core performs on whatever the [campaign](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/) and the [upper ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) hand you, which lets you start endgame farming earlier than most classes. Farm [wormholes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) and [Uber](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) content the moment your survival allows it — volume feeds the drone-scaling slots first.

### Phase 2 — the market closes the gaps

Two purchases define the mid-game: the piece that makes your drone cap or tempo feel complete, and the survival piece that stops endgame content from one-shotting you. Both are [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) questions before they are luck questions — which makes them [gold-income](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) questions, and Season 10's economy rewards exactly that planning.

### Phase 3 — the Codex project

Once the build is stable, the [Codex of Eternity](/guides/hero-siege-codex-guide/) is where targeted crafting finishes the drone-scaling slots. Converting farmed materials into attempts at the exact affixes your automation scales with is the same "printing" loop every endgame build runs — the drone Marksman just aims it at fewer, more defined targets.

## When a Drone Marksman Slot Is Done

The automation makes "done" unusually measurable: watch your clear speed, not your gear score.

- **Drone-scaling slots** are done when pushing one more [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) floor no longer needs better drones — the army clears what you can survive.
- **Your survivability slots** are done when endgame content's burst no longer threatens the run — you should die to decisions, not to pack contact.
- **Everything else** stays whatever dropped last, until a [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) listing or a farming streak upgrades it for free.

## Marksman BiS FAQ

### Is the drone build still strong after balance patches?

It has survived every season by design — automation builds are a fixture of the class. Individual drone breakpoints move with patches, but the build's core loop and gearing logic are structural, not numeric.

### Should I mix drone scaling with personal attack stats?

Only in the transition phase, where personal damage patches over a not-yet-complete army. Past early endgame, mixed investment serves neither half of the build — commit the slots to the automation.

### What is the most common Marksman gearing mistake?

Neglecting personal survivability because the drones "do the fighting." The army keeps shooting while you reposition — but every death stops the farm, and farm volume is the whole engine of this build's progression.
