## The Tier You Actually Play In

Every loot game has a tier that lives in a weird spot: not the celebrated peak, but the gear that fills endgame builds while players chase that peak. In Hero Siege, that tier is Satanic. Above it sit the headline rarities — [Angelic](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) and [Unholy](/items/hero-siege-unholy-items/) — but open almost any endgame build's gear tab in [Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) and Satanic pieces are doing the daily work inside it.

<Callout type="info" title="Community-documented tier">
Hero Siege publishes no official rarity tables. The tier relationships described here reflect current endgame practice and guide consensus; roll ranges and drop rates deserve in-game verification, especially with 7.0.x patches still landing.
</Callout>

## What Satanic Pieces Do for a Build

The practical value of the tier is reliability. A [Heroic](/items/hero-siege-heroic-items/) piece gets a build through the mid-ladder; a Satanic piece is where affixes start looking endgame-serious — enough power that the slot stops being a priority upgrade target for a while. For most builds, the realistic gear arc looks like this:

1. **Leveling drops** — anything that clears the [campaign acts](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/).
2. **Mid-tier uniques** — the broad [unique pool](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) filling slots with useful affixes.
3. **Satanic core** — the endgame spine of the build, farmed in volume.
4. **Peak-tier upgrades** — [Angelic and Unholy](/items/hero-siege-unholy-items/) pieces replacing individual Satanic slots as they arrive.

The arc matters because it reframes what "Satanic farming" means: not a detour from chasing peak tiers, but the phase of the season where your build becomes endgame-capable at all.

## Where Satanic Farming Happens

Satanic drops concentrate where endgame farming concentrates — no surprise, since the tier exists to reward exactly that play:

- **[Wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pushes** — the community's deep-progression loop, and a reliable source of high-tier loot pressure.
- **[Uber encounters](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/)** — the game's superboss tier and its best loot checks.
- **High-difficulty routes** — anything at the top of the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/), where the game's reward scaling lives.

Volume is the mechanic, as with every top-band tier: a build that clears fast turns farming hours into drop chances, which is why [speed-clearing class picks](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) indirectly farm Satanic pieces faster too.

## Satanic and the Market

Not every Satanic slot needs to drop for you. The [reworked Tarethiel Market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) made buying single-slot upgrades a standard part of the gear arc — and Satanic pieces are the tier where purchasing genuinely competes with farming, because their market prices sit in "earnable this week" territory rather than season-milestone territory.

The [gold farming](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) routine exists partly for this: a steady gold income converts a missing Satanic slot into a solved problem without waiting on luck.

## Satanic FAQ

### How many Satanic pieces does an endgame build need?

However many slots lack a peak-tier answer — commonly most of them for most of the season. A full Satanic core with one or two [Angelic](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) upgrades is a strong endgame position in Season 10.

### Should I upgrade a Satanic piece or replace it?

Replace-if-you-can, upgrade-if-you-must. The game's upgrade systems touch items in different ways, and the community's advice varies by item type — verify upgrade outcomes in-game before spending materials you farmed for a specific slot.

### Do Satanic items drop in Nightmare?

Top-band drops belong to the upper ladder. Nightmare is where builds are assembled ([the reworked tier's role](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/)); Hell and Inferno are where its serious farming happens.
