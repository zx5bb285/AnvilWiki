## The Curse You Hold the Bottle For

Uber [Amun Ra](/bosses/hero-siege-amun-ra-boss/) decides his fight with a curse on a timer: purify it or watch most of your health bar evaporate. Amun Ra's Demise, the flask, takes that story and flips who is holding it. It is a Tier SS Heroic bottle named for the boss's death — not his power — and community databases document it as granting an active effect that carries his name. Whatever the effect's internals do, the identity is unmistakable: this is the piece of the tomb you put in your potion slot, the one item from the [modern Uber layer](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/)'s orbit where the boss's own mythology ends up on your keybind instead of your corpse run.

## What Does the Flask Actually Do?

Three documented facts, in decreasing order of certainty:

- **It grants +1 to the Amun Ra's Demise active effect.** The flask raises a same-named active skill — the item and the effect share one identity, which is why the name reads like a boss card.
- **Community-documented timings: a 30-second cooldown and a 15-second duration.** If those hold, re-casting the moment the cooldown clears gives roughly half uptime — a generous figure for an on-use item, and the practical reason it competes for the potion slot rather than sitting forgotten in a stash tab.
- **What the effect does inside those 15 seconds is the part to verify.** This page deliberately does not restate community summaries of the effect's internals. The in-game tooltip is the source of truth, and active-effect tuning is precisely what hotfixes like to touch.

## Amun Ra's Demise or Sung Lee's Flask of Carnage?

The potion slot is where Hero Siege hides real power, and its unique-flask bench is deep. Two names currently headline it, and they are not really competitors — they are answers to different questions:

| | Amun Ra's Demise | [Sung Lee's Flask of Carnage](/items/hero-siege-sung-lees-flask-of-carnage/) |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | An active effect on a cooldown | [Sung Lee](/bosses/hero-siege-sung-lee-boss/)'s signature bleed, always on |
| Documented hook | +1 to the namesake effect; community-documented 30s cooldown, 15s duration | +1 to +5 to Momentary Insanity; all attacks cause bleeding |
| Job | A button you press when it matters | A passive the game plays for you |

An always-on attack rider multiplies everything you do and never asks for management; an active effect concentrates power into windows you choose. Which one wins depends on whether your build is starving for constant amplification or already has a rotation that benefits from timed bursts. There is also an access asymmetry worth naming: both are endgame-tier drops tied to their respective bosses, so for most characters the debate resolves itself to whichever drops first — and the loser of the theoretical argument still beats an empty potion slot.

## What Does Getting One Cost?

The drop source is the boss himself. Community drop databases list the flask from Amun Ra on Inferno difficulty — the Uber version of the fight, which sits behind the longest unlock chain of the [modern trio](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/): Pyramid Floor 1, a Curacan Key, and the Ankh of Eternal Death built from fifty Scrolls of Ra, an Angelic Key and two Enchanted Sigils. The [boss guide](/bosses/hero-siege-amun-ra-boss/) walks the whole chain and the fight's purification exam; the short version is that the flask's real price is the tomb run, not the kill.

If the run stalls, the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) is the legitimate shortcut — modern-layer items trade there, and buying one is a decision about [gold income](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) versus farm time, not a moral one. For context on where the flask sits in the wider item system, the [uniques overview](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) maps the tiers above and below it.

## Amun Ra's Demise FAQ

### Can I craft or upgrade the flask?

Honestly: not clearly documented. The crafting cube's dust system upgrades items within their tier, but community sources do not state cleanly whether this flask participates — and [Sung Lee's flask](/items/hero-siege-sung-lees-flask-of-carnage/) is documented as non-forgeable, which is a warning against assuming the whole flask category crafts. Test with cheap inputs before committing anything rare.

### Is the flask a key or requirement for the tomb?

No. The tomb's gates are the Curacan Key and the Ankh of Eternal Death; the flask is loot, not a key item. Nothing in the community documentation ties the flask to opening, entering or clearing the fight — the confusion is understandable given the shared name, but the unlock chain does not involve it.

### How do I confirm the current cooldown and duration?

Read the tooltip on the flask in your own inventory, after any significant patch. Community pages — including the numbers on this one — snapshot a moment in the patch cycle, and active-effect tuning is a recurring target of hotfixes. The tooltip is the only source that is current by definition.
