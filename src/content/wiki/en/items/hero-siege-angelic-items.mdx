## The Milestone Item of a Hero Siege Season

Ask a Hero Siege player how their season is going and the answer, sooner or later, is a rarity: whether the first Angelic has landed. Angelic items occupy the top slice of the game's [rarity ladder](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/), alongside [Unholy](/items/hero-siege-unholy-items/) — and because the game's power ceiling lives in these tiers, the first Angelic drop or craft of a season is treated less like an item and more like an event.

That culture is worth understanding before the mechanics, because it tells you what you are actually chasing: not a flat stat upgrade, but the point where your build stops compromising.

<Callout type="info" title="What this page can and cannot promise">
Drop rates for Angelic items are not officially published, and exact numbers in older guides may predate Season 10. What follows is the community's tested route map — the destinations are real, the exact odds are not knowable from outside the game.
</Callout>

## What Makes the Angelic Tier Different

Within Hero Siege's top rarity slice, Angelic pieces share the traits every endgame chase item has — strong implicit power, top-range affixes — but the tier's practical meaning is simpler: Angelic items are where a build's affixes stop being compromises. The difference between a [Heroic-tier](/items/hero-siege-heroic-items/) slot and an Angelic one is usually the difference between "this stat is decent" and "this stat is exactly what my build scales with."

That is also why Angelics anchor the [BiS conversation](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) for every class: best-in-slot lists are, in practice, lists of the slots where an Angelic exists for your build.

## The Two Routes to a First Angelic

### Route 1: Drops, with volume

The straightforward path: play endgame content at volume and let the loot filter do its work. Wormhole pushes, [Uber kills](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) and high-difficulty farming sessions are where top-tier drops live — the same [difficulty rule](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) that governs all Hero Siege farming applies doubly at the top of the rarity ladder.

The honest framing from the community: drop-chasing works, but it is a lottery with good odds only at high volume. Bring a build that clears fast, because [kill speed](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) is the only lever you control.

### Route 2: The Codex

The controllable path. The [Codex of Eternity](/guides/hero-siege-codex-guide/) converts farmed materials into targeted crafting attempts, and steering affixes toward a specific slot beats waiting for the same slot to drop perfectly. Top players who seem to "print" Angelics are almost always running this loop — farm volume, craft attempts, keep the hits.

## What to Do While Chasing

The chase for a first Angelic takes long enough that how you spend it matters:

- **Farm what pays regardless.** [Gold](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) and relic progress compound whether or not the drop arrives.
- **Watch the market.** Other players' finds surface on the [Tarethiel Market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) — a listed Angelic in your build's slot sometimes costs less than the hours to farm one.
- **Upgrade around the slot.** An Angelic lands in one slot; the rest of your [unique](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) setup keeps improving meanwhile.

## Angelic FAQ

### Angelic or Unholy — which is "better"?

They are the two headline tiers of the top slice, and which one is "best" depends on the item and the build rather than the color of the rarity. Both mark the ceiling of what gear contributes to a build.

### Do Angelics drop in every difficulty?

Top-tier drops concentrate in high-difficulty, endgame content. Farming for Angelics below the upper ladder is spending time on odds the game does not offer.

### Should new players care about Angelics?

As a direction, yes; as a goal for week one, no. The ladder to a first Angelic runs through levels, build completion and the rest of the [item system](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) — the tier is the season's finish line, not its starting block.
