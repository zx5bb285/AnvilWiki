## What Is a Boss's Signature Doing in a Potion Slot?

Some Hero Siege items are, in effect, a boss fight you get to keep. **Sung Lee's Flask of Carnage** is the purest example: a **level 100 potion** carrying the War God's name, his temperament, and — in spirit — his weapon rack. It drops from [War God Sung Lee](/bosses/hero-siege-sung-lee-boss/) himself, the classic trio's positioning exam, which means the item's acquisition story and its power fantasy point at the same fight.

The documented effect line is short. **All attacks cause enemies to bleed**, and the flask rolls **+1 to +5 to Momentary Insanity**. Everything interesting about the item is packed into those two clauses, so this page takes them one at a time.

## Why "All Attacks Bleed" Is the Whole Item

Most damage bonuses attach to a stat you already build or a skill you already cast. The flask's signature effect attaches to the act of attacking — every attack, from anything, causes bleeding. That is why the item reads as a universal amplifier rather than a build-defining unique: whatever your archetype swings, hurls or channels, the effect rides along.

The practical consequences:

- **Fast-hitting builds squeeze the most out of it.** If your rotation lands many small hits, the bleed clause triggers on all of them, so attack-speed-oriented melee and rapid-cast builds get the largest surface area from one item.
- **It does not ask you to change anything.** No new rotation, no stat threshold, no reroll. A potion that upgrades every attack you were making anyway is about the cheapest integration an item can offer.
- **How hard it hits is the undocumented part.** The bleed's exact damage scaling is not something community sources pin down reliably, so treat this page's framing as the strategic read, not a formula — verify the actual numbers on your own character sheet in-game.

The **Momentary Insanity** roll is the build-specific half. Its value depends on what that skill does for your class, and since the flask rolls a range (+1 to +5), two identical flasks are not identical items. Check the skill before you commit; a big roll on a skill your build ignores is just flavor.

## How Do You Get a Flask You Cannot Craft?

This is where the item gets its reputation. Community documentation is blunt: **the Flask of Carnage cannot be crafted.** There is no recipe, no crafting-cube route, no material grind that ends in a guaranteed one. War God Sung Lee is the source, full stop.

Practically, that means the route is the fight:

1. Get to the **Eternal Battlefields** — a portal that appears at random on Hell difficulty.
2. Pay the **50 Battle Fragment** toll, and keep a surplus so a spawn never has to be skipped.
3. Clear the fight — which is a distance-discipline exam, not a damage race — and repeat.

The full breakdown of Earthquake, the spike-ball bounce and Rain of Ruin lives in the [Sung Lee boss guide](/bosses/hero-siege-sung-lee-boss/); the item page's job is the honest summary: the flask is a boss-drop exclusive gated behind a toll and a skill check. He is worth farming regardless — he sits on the classic trio's standard weekly rotation — but tunnel-visioning one drop is how players burn out on otherwise profitable bosses.

## Flask of Carnage or Amun Ra's Demise: Who Owns the Potion Slot?

The potion slot's real competition is the other boss-signature consumable. [Amun Ra's Demise](/items/hero-siege-amun-ras-demise/) is a **Heroic flask, Tier SS**, that grants **+1 to its namesake active** — community documentation describes a roughly 30-second cooldown with a 15-second duration — and drops from the long unlock chain behind [Uber Amun Ra](/bosses/hero-siege-amun-ra-boss/).

| Contender | Documented effect | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flask of Carnage | All attacks cause bleeding; +1 to +5 to Momentary Insanity | War God Sung Lee |
| Amun Ra's Demise | +1 to its namesake active; about 30s cooldown, 15s duration | Uber Amun Ra |

The honest answer to "which one" is that they do different jobs. Carnage is a passive amplifier — it changes what every attack does and then stops asking for attention. Amun Ra's Demise is an active button — a periodic effect you press, on a cooldown you manage. Packs and sustained fights reward the passive; builds that want another cooldown layer want the button. Where the two sit in the wider [loot structure](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) is a question of tier and availability as much as power, so verify both in-game before crowning one.

## Flask of Carnage FAQ

### Does the bleed stack with other damage-over-time effects?

Community sources do not document the stacking rules reliably, so this page will not invent them. Treat bleed interaction with other DoT effects as an in-game verification task — a few minutes with a training dummy answers it better than any secondhand claim.

### Do I need to be level 100 to use the flask?

Yes. It is documented as a level 100 potion, which makes it an endgame item by gate as well as by source: you meet its level requirement and its boss at roughly the same point in a season.

### Is it worth farming Sung Lee just for this flask?

Rarely as a sole goal, easily as a bonus. Sung Lee is already on the classic trio's standard weekly rotation because his drops feed endgame builds, so the flask is best treated as one more reason the rotation pays — not a slot machine you feed Battle Fragments until it blinks.
