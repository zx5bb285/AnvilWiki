## Why Carry the Boss's Own Weapon?

Looting the boss's own weapon off the floor is the oldest promise an ARPG makes, and **War God's Lance** is Hero Siege making good on it: a **Satanic polearm** named after [War God Sung Lee](/bosses/hero-siege-sung-lee-boss/), the classic Uber trio's positioning exam. A boss famous for hurling weapons at you leaves behind a weapon of his own — the theming is the marketing, and it works.

One honesty note before anything else: community documentation ties this item to the War God, but the exact current drop attribution is exactly the kind of detail that shifts between patches. Verify the live source in-game rather than trusting any single database page, including this one.

## What Does the Weapon Actually Roll? Handle With Care

<Callout type="warn" title="The famous numbers are Season 5 history">
Every specific stat value you will find quoted for War God's Lance comes from the Season 5 era, preserved in community wiki snapshots that largely stopped updating in late 2024 — before Season 9's reworks and all of Season 10. This page therefore describes the weapon by category only. If a page quotes exact figures for this lance as current fact, that page is older than it looks.
</Callout>

With that warning on record, the documented stat identity has five categories:

- **Enhanced damage** — a multiplier riding on the weapon's own output, the classic melee headline line.
- **Attack speed** — the stat that converts sustained contact into sustained damage.
- **Crushing blow** — the anti-tank modifier melee players prize against fat endgame health bars.
- **A boost to all attributes at once** — a flat, class-agnostic line that never has a dead roll.
- **Damage that scales with your level** — a "based on Level" line, the kind that made legacy guides love the weapon: it grows as you do.

Read as a shape rather than a list, that is a sustained-melee pressure weapon: it pays you for staying in range and swinging, and its scaling line keeps it relevant as your character climbs. Whether any of those five categories still rolls the same values in Season 10 is precisely what the warning above refuses to promise.

## Who Actually Wants a Polearm?

The lance's identity points at builds that live inside the pack rather than at its edge:

- **The [Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/)** is the obvious first look. A whirlwind-style core that converts weapon damage into constant clear speed wants exactly the pairing the lance documents — weapon-scaled enhanced damage plus attack speed — and its face-forward fighting style keeps the crushing-blow pressure on the biggest target in the pack.
- **The [Shield Lancer](/guides/hero-siege-shield-lancer-build/)** is the second. Sustained contact is the whole build, and an all-attributes line supports the defensive side of that identity instead of forcing a pure-offense trade.

Whether the lance is best-in-slot for either class in Season 10 is a per-patch question this page will not answer with numbers it cannot verify. The slot-priority method on those class pages survives balance churn; individual items do not, and that includes this one.

## Where Does the Lance Drop Now?

The thematic answer and the practical answer point the same way: at Sung Lee. Reaching him means the classic trio route — a portal that appears at random on Hell difficulty, a **50 Battle Fragment** toll, and the Eternal Battlefields fight itself, which tests distance discipline rather than raw damage. The mechanics live in the [boss guide](/bosses/hero-siege-sung-lee-boss/); for the weapon, treat the fight as the documented home turf and confirm the drop in-game as part of your first kill.

As a **Satanic-tier** piece, the lance sits on the tier that most builds call home for most of a season — the [Satanic tier guide](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) covers where that tier's pieces come from and why the tier stays relevant deep into the endgame.

The last practical note is about the farming loop, not the lance: Sung Lee is on the classic trio's standard weekly rotation, which means the weapon shares its farm with everything else that rotation pays. A themed weapon you were one weekly kill away from anyway is a much better chase than a single-drop lottery — compare Aimbot.exe's [million-to-one reality](/items/hero-siege-aimbot-exe/) and the difference in acquisition philosophies writes itself.

## War God's Lance FAQ

### Is War God's Lance still good in Season 10?

The honest answer is that its identity probably survives and its numbers may not. Category-level appeal — enhanced damage, attack speed, crushing blow, all-attributes, level scaling — is why the weapon keeps a reputation; the actual rolls on a Season 10 copy are something only your inventory can confirm. Check before you build a season around it.

### Do I need War God's Lance to clear Sung Lee?

No — and the reverse is the right order. The fight is a positioning exam: damage alone does not clear him, reading the arena does. The lance is a souvenir that happens to be strong, not a key to the encounter, so do not postpone learning the fight while hunting the trophy.

### Does the weapon type matter for build fit, or just the stats?

Both matter, and only testing settles the mix. Polearm handling — reach and swing pattern — interacts with each class's skills differently, so the same stat line can play better or worse depending on how your skills trigger attacks. Equip one, run your usual pack, and judge the feel alongside the numbers.
