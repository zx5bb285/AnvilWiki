## A Signature Drop You Identify by Its Address

Most signature items introduce themselves with a stat line. Leviathan's Blood introduces itself with an address: community databases place it on [Phantom Leviathan](/bosses/hero-siege-phantom-leviathan/), one of the three new Uber bosses [Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) added with the Ebontharn launch. The address comes with a rank — Heroic rarity, Tier SS, level 100 — and that combination is the settled part of the item's story. Weeks into a live season, the settled part is also the useful part, so this page is about what the address and the rank tell you, and where honest uncertainty begins.

<Callout type="info" title="What this page commits to">
The drop source, tier and level gate are the community-documented backbone. The piece's exact roll lines move with balance patches and are deliberately not fixed here — read the tooltip in-game before spending gold or farm time on one.
</Callout>

## Why Does the Season's 24-Item Pool Matter?

Season 10's [loot headline](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) was twenty-four new items, all Heroic rarity at Tier SS with level-100 requirements, split across the three new Ubers. The split is thematic, and the community's reading of it has been consistent since launch:

| New Uber | Pool theme (community-described) | Natural fit |
|---|---|---|
| [Blood Maiden](/bosses/hero-siege-blood-maiden/) | Damage-over-time, parasites, summons | DoT and pet-leaning builds |
| [Captain Grimtide](/bosses/hero-siege-captain-grimtide/) | Physical, melee | Front-line melee classes |
| [Phantom Leviathan](/bosses/hero-siege-phantom-leviathan/) | Generic, survivability | Everything else — which is most builds |

That third row is why one item from this branch earns a page of its own. Blood Maiden's pool flatters specific build families, and Grimtide's speaks to melee; the Leviathan branch, as community guides describe it, is the one nobody is locked out of. When a pool's identity is "useful to nearly everyone," each piece in it inherits that property — subject, always, to what actually rolled on the drop.

## What Does a Generic, Survivability-Themed Pool Do for a Build?

A themed drop asks your build to be something. A generic one does not. Community write-ups of the new layer have framed the Leviathan pieces — the Ribcage most prominently — as the entry point any reasonably built character can use without reshaping a single slot around them, and Leviathan's Blood inherits that framing as a member of the same branch.

Two honest limits come with it:

- **Generic does not mean automatic.** Whether this piece beats what is already in the slot is a roll-by-roll question. Community guidance on the new pool says so directly: a well-rolled older item with the right affixes can outvalue a fresh Tier SS piece with poor ones. The season's newest label is not a stat.
- **Survivability value is quiet.** Durability never shows up in a damage screenshot, which is exactly why generic-defensive pieces get undersold in week-one discussions and reappraised later, when the content starts denting the builds that skipped them.

## How Do You Farm the Phantom Leviathan Pool?

The address is the top of the [Uber roster](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/), so the farm route is the endgame route:

1. **Complete the build first.** The new layer assumes a finished [endgame core](/items/hero-siege-heroic-items/) — the [transition phase](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) is what you finish before this farm starts paying.
2. **Add the new trio to the weekly rotation.** The [Phantom Leviathan guide](/bosses/hero-siege-phantom-leviathan/) covers readiness and what the community has documented about the fight so far.
3. **Let volume work.** No source this page trusts publishes a per-boss drop rate for the new items, so treat any percentage you see elsewhere as a guess and let weekly kills do the sampling.

Two practical notes. First, the whole S10 Uber pool is level-100-gated — this is an endgame prize, not a leveling waypoint. Second, the new layer doubles as the season's best general loot source, so the rotation funds itself even across dry streaks.

## Should You Buy Instead of Farm?

Early in a season, signature drops carry headline prices, and the new Uber pools are no exception on the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/). The generic-themed branch changes the arithmetic slightly: because the Leviathan pieces suit so many builds, demand is broad, which keeps early prices high — but the same breadth means a substitute piece from the same pool may serve your slot nearly as well at a fraction of the headline. The [market guide](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/)'s discipline applies: price the roll, not the reputation, and remember the pool keeps dropping every week while a bad purchase does not refund itself.

## Leviathan's Blood FAQ

### Do I need level 100 to equip it?

Yes, per community databases — every item in the Season 10 Uber pool carries the same level-100 requirement. The piece is designed to land in an endgame build, not to carry you toward one.

### How does the Leviathan pool compare with the other two new pools?

Blood Maiden's skews toward damage-over-time, parasites and summons; Grimtide's toward physical and melee. Leviathan's is the generic, survivability-themed branch — the pool community guides point at first for builds that belong to no particular theme, which is most of them.

### Why doesn't this page list the item's exact stats?

Because the season is live and the patch cadence is active — roll lines on new items are exactly what hotfixes retune. Fixing numbers here would age the page faster than the game ages the item. The in-game tooltip is the source of truth; the address, the tier and the pool structure are what stay true.
