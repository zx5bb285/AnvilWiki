## What Does Aimbot.exe Actually Do?

Every loot game eventually prints an item whose name is a joke and whose power is not. In Hero Siege that item is **Aimbot.exe** — a **Satanic charm** whose entire effect is one line: **+1 to Aimbot**, which makes all of your casts and attacks automatically target the nearest enemy.

Read that again with the joke in mind. It does not add damage, crit, or a multiplier. It deletes a category of player error: the missed click, the cast that sailed past a strafing enemy, the attack you had to babysit onto a target. In practice that means your hands stay on movement while the game handles pointing, your clears never stall on a stray shot, and cast-heavy builds keep their rhythm inside packs instead of fighting the reticle.

The community's favorite part is the flavor text — **"So far undetected by VAC.."** — which is exactly the joke you think it is. The item plays by the game's rules; only the name cheats.

## Why Is a Targeting Convenience Rated SS?

Because at the top end, quality of life is power. A damage stat helps the attacks you land; reliable targeting makes sure every attack lands. That difference compounds with everything else your build does — more casts connect, more attacks find a target while you are busy dodging, and none of your uptime is spent steering.

There is also a slot argument. Aimbot.exe is a charm, not a weapon or armor piece, so its effect stacks on top of whatever your damage slots are already doing. Items that multiply the value of your other equipment age better than items that ask you to rebuild around them, and community [tier lists](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) have kept this one at **Tier SS** for that reason: it does one small thing, and the one small thing helps all the big things.

The honest caveat: how much it helps is build-dependent. If your skills already track targets on their own, the overlap is real and the upgrade shrinks. Verify what your own rotation wastes before treating it as mandatory.

## How Rare Is It, Honestly?

Here is the part that keeps Aimbot.exe a story rather than a shopping item. Community-documented drop figures put it at:

- **1 in 3,406,750** — the overall drop rate attached to the item.
- **1 in 1,081,643** — the rate inside its drop zones, which is better, in the way that one lottery ticket is better than three.

<Callout type="warn" title="A drop rate is a description, not a promise">
These numbers come from community databases, not official patch notes, and no pity system is documented to soften them. The correct mental model is "it can happen," not "it will happen at X kills." Anyone selling you a guaranteed farm route for this charm is selling you a schedule for disappointment.
</Callout>

That honesty matters in both directions. Yes, the odds are absurd. No, they are not zero — and the short version of why people keep hunting is that the item's story is part of its value. Owning one is a conversation; seeing one drop in someone else's stream is a core memory.

## So Can You Farm for It at All?

You can put yourself in its way, which is not the same as farming it. Aimbot.exe drops from the **Unstable Rift**, and rift runs are already among the best experience-per-hour activities in the game — high risk, high density, worth your evenings on their own merits. That is the healthy framing: run rifts because rifts pay you anyway, and let the charm be a tail-risk bonus on top of a plan that already works.

The other route is patience with someone else's lottery. Uniques circulate, so it is worth checking whether one is listed on the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) before committing weeks of rift runs — a single purchase can close a slot that luck refuses to. The broader point generalizes to the whole chase tier: drop rates in Hero Siege are the least-published part of its [itemization](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/), so any acquisition plan built on a single rare drop is a plan built on weather.

If you are assembling a Satanic-tier setup and want this charm's neighbors, the [Satanic tier guide](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) maps where that tier's pieces come from and which ones are realistic to target.

## Aimbot.exe FAQ

### Is Aimbot.exe worth it if my skills already auto-target?

Partly — and only in-game testing settles it. Overlap is the whole question: the more your rotation tracks targets by itself, the less a global auto-target adds. Players with manual-aim gaps get the most value; players with fully homing kits should compare carefully before paying anything for it.

### Should I grind Unstable Rifts specifically until it drops?

No — grind rifts because they are excellent experience and loot, and count any Aimbot.exe as an accident. A million-to-one drop is not a goal, it is a backdrop; the moment it becomes your goal, the grind has stopped paying for itself.

### Is the VAC flavor text an actual cheat reference?

It is the item's documented flavor line, and the joke is deliberate — "VAC" riffs on anti-cheat software, as if the item itself were an undetected aimbot. It is flavor only. The item's real effect is the in-game auto-target, and it has nothing to do with cheating.
