## A Class That Refuses to Stand Still

Every Hero Siege class has one idea at its center. The Nomad's idea is **that standing still is the only real mistake**. Its kit, its talents, and its damage loop all assume continuous movement — you fire on the move, re-angle before packs collapse on your old position, and treat movement speed as a stat that pays out twice: once as survival, once as damage tempo.

That design makes the Nomad the natural pick for players who already like the flow of a mobile ranged playstyle, and Season 10's tier lists (LagoFast and MetaRoad both place it in the strong starter band) reflect a class in good shape after the talent rework. If your instinct in every ARPG is "kite everything," the Nomad will feel like home; if your instinct is to stand and trade, read the [Pyromancer](/guides/hero-siege-pyromancer-build/) page instead — it rewards the opposite instinct.

## The Hit-and-Run Loop

The Nomad's combat is a two-beat rhythm, and the beat length is your difficulty dial:

1. **Strike from an angle.** Open on a pack from the side or behind — ranged attacks from unexpected directions buy you the first volley before threats orient.
2. **Relocate before the response.** Move to a new firing position while the pack turns. The Nomad's mobility nodes make this second beat fast enough that most packs never get their answer off.

Against single targets, the same loop compresses: fire in windows, rotate between them, and use the map's terrain to break line-of-sight between exchanges. Players coming from stationary builds should practice the loop in Normal acts — the [campaign boss ladder](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/) is a safe classroom for it.

## Talents: Speed Is a Damage Stat

The Nomad's tree rewards an unusual priority order:

1. **Movement and attack-on-the-move nodes first.** Anything that lets you fire while relocating converts your defense stat into uptime, and uptime into damage.
2. **Ranged damage multipliers second.** Once the loop is fluid, broad ranged-damage nodes compound across every volley.
3. **A thin survival band third.** Speed is your armor; a small health and mitigation band covers the moments a corner takes that away.
4. **Tempo nodes last.** Cooldown-adjacent picks that shorten the gap between strike-and-relocate cycles.

The synergy with seasonal systems is strong: fast builds extract more from every [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) branch, because density nodes pay out per minute and the Nomad sees more minutes of action per hour than slower classes.

## Gear: Building for Tempo

- **Movement speed affixes** are the build's signature stat — community advice is to treat them as damage rolls, not utility rolls.
- **Ranged weapon damage and attack-speed** keep the "strike" beat of the loop lethal.
- **Defensive pieces** cover the exception cases (cornered fights, boss arenas); the [market guide](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) covers filling these gaps without overpaying.

Unique items with on-move or tempo-triggered effects are the class's luxury tier — watch the [uniques overview](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) for what the tiers mean as your season progresses.

## Endgame: Where Mobility Pays Its Rent

The Nomad's value curve rises with content difficulty, because harder content punishes standing still harder:

- **[Wormholes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/):** deep levels spawn dense, fast-responding packs — a class that never gets touched by the response outscales heavier builds on time-per-level.
- **[Uber fights](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/):** attack-dodge-exchange patterns reward the same repositioning reflex the whole build trains.
- **[Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/):** claim movement-adjacent and defensive nodes early; the offensive branches ride in on your gear curve.

## FAQ

**Nomad or Marksman for a mobile ranged class?**
The [Marksman](/guides/hero-siege-marksman-build/) delegates aiming to drones and rewards routing; the Nomad keeps you doing everything yourself and rewards active repositioning. Try both to level 20 — they feel nothing alike.

**Is the Nomad's damage competitive at the top end?**
Season 10 tier coverage puts it in the strong band; its ceiling rewards players who never stop moving, and drops off for stationary playstyles.

**What kills Nomads?**
Corners and tunnel vision — the moments the loop breaks. The fix is map habit, not more defense nodes.

**How does the Nomad perform in co-op?**
Well, with one adjustment: coordinate who holds monster attention. A melee ally like a [Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) anchoring packs gives the Nomad free angles.
