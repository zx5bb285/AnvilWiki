## A Class That Fights Ahead of Itself

Most Hero Siege classes express themselves through the moment of impact. A totem class does the opposite: it makes an investment before the fight, then lives with what it built. The Shaman's community-documented identity is exactly that — a totem build where the player drops totems and then operates around them.

This is why Shaman discussions read differently from discussions of almost any other class. Nobody argues about the best combo. They argue about placement, timing, and whether the totems were already doing the work before the player arrived.

Totems are the documented backbone of the class, and that is what this guide is built on. The playstyle argument below does not depend on those specifics.

## The Setup-Then-Fight Loop

The Shaman's rhythm has three beats, and the third one is what players underestimate.

1. **Invest.** Place the totems before contact. This is the planning half of the class.
2. **Anchor.** Fight inside the area the totems define, rather than chasing kills across the screen.
3. **Re-invest.** Rebuild when you move. Movement costs a totem class more than it costs a class that simply keeps casting.

That third beat is the entire skill curve. Every class has to reposition; a totem class has to reposition *and* re-place. Learning to read when a fight is worth rebuilding for — and when the right answer is to walk on — is the difference between a Shaman that clears smoothly and one that feels like it is always catching up.

## Totems Versus Active-Skill Classes

Put the two side by side and the trade becomes obvious:

| | Active-skill class | Totem class (Shaman) |
|---|---|---|
| Where damage comes from | Buttons pressed in the fight | Setup placed before the fight |
| What it rewards | Reaction speed | Positioning and foresight |
| What it punishes | Hesitation | Fighting on the move |
| Failure mode | Missing the moment | Being caught with the field unprepared |

Neither column is better. A [Stormweaver](/guides/hero-siege-stormweaver-build/) shapes a fight by swinging at it; a Shaman shapes it by choosing where the fight will happen. The honest self-test: when a group of monsters appears, is your instinct to close the distance or to claim the ground? Shaman players answer the second.

## Where the Totem Rhythm Breaks Down

Two situations punish setup-heavy classes harder than they punish anyone else, and both are worth knowing before you commit a season:

- **Bosses that relocate.** A build that performs best inside a prepared area loses the most when the arena forces movement. Boss encounters that scatter the fight are the Shaman's real difficulty check, not the ones with the biggest damage numbers — the [Uber fights](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) are the honest test.
- **Farming on autopilot.** Totem classes are strong in content you can plan around and slightly tedious in content you want to blast. That is a real cost in a season where the [farming loop](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) funds everything else.

Knowing this before you invest is the point. Players who quit on a totem class usually quit over the second one.

## Starting a Shaman Season

- **Campaign to level 100.** Level with the totem loop turned on. The acts are a forgiving place to build the placement habit, and the [beginner guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) covers the mistakes that apply to every class.
- **Test on the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/).** Mid-ladder is where you find out whether your setup speed keeps up with the fights you are choosing.
- **Give the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) a single focus.** Setup classes gain the least from scattering investment; the [league starter page](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) shows what a committed shell looks like.
- **Accept the mobility tax.** Build your habits around the rebuild, not against it.

## Shaman FAQ

### Does the Shaman have to be a totem build?

Totems are the class's documented identity, and every community description of the Shaman starts there. Season 10 material detailed enough to describe an alternative has not been written yet, so this page will not pretend one exists.

### Is the Shaman a good pick for a first season?

It is a reasonable one if the setup description appeals to you, and a poor one if it does not — the class asks for patience that a player looking for constant action will not enjoy giving. Other Season 9 free picks like the [Samurai](/guides/hero-siege-samurai-build/) and [Demonspawn](/guides/hero-siege-demonspawn-build/) have their own documented rhythms if this one does not fit.

### How does the Shaman compare on the Season 10 tier list?

Check the [class tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) for the current ranking, but read it as a snapshot. Totem classes tend to rank conservatively on lists built around raw clear speed, because their strength shows up in prepared content that a tier list cannot easily measure.
