## For Players Who Die in Other ARPGs

Every loot game has players who bounce off it — not for lack of interest, but because squishy characters die to the same pack three times and the fun drains out. [Season 10's](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) free-class expansion has a pick for exactly those players: the **Shield Lancer**, a class whose community shorthand is that defense is part of the offense, not a tax on it.

Alongside [Marauder](/guides/hero-siege-marauder-build/), [Plague Doctor](/guides/hero-siege-plague-doctor-build/) and [Jötunn](/guides/hero-siege-jotunn-build/), it joined the free roster this season — which removed the last excuse not to try it.

## What Defense-Forward Buys a New Player

The [beginner guide's](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) most common levying mistakes — over-farming side zones, hoarding gear, dying to the same boss repeatedly — all get cheaper on a durable class. A first [campaign](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/) on a Shield Lancer teaches the same lessons as any class, with fewer deaths per lesson.

The class's endgame case rests on the same trait. Hero Siege's hardest content — [deep wormholes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/), the [Uber roster](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) — punishes paper builds, which is why [durability slots](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) sit so high on every endgame [BiS map](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/). A class that brings durability as an identity starts with that tax pre-paid and spends its gear budget on the damage plan instead.

## How a First Shield Lancer Season Goes

1. **Campaign first, curiosity second.** Clear the acts, reach [level 100](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/), and resist the side-quest trap — the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) is the real game.
2. **Learn what your defense already covers.** The class survives mid-ladder content that deletes other picks — use that margin to farm [Nightmare and Hell](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) earlier than you think you can.
3. **Commit the talent and Ether points to one damage plan.** Defense-forward does not mean damage-free; the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) and [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) reward a committed identity on every class.
4. **Endgame like anyone else.** [League starter](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) routines, [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pushes, [Uber](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) rotations and the [gold economy](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) that funds [top-tier slots](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) — the class plays all of it.

## Shield Lancer FAQ

### Shield Lancer or Viking for a first character?

Both are durable, free-tier-adjacent answers — the Viking is the [community's long-standing default](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) with the deepest guide coverage, while the Shield Lancer is the fresh free option with less written material. Try both in the first hour; the one whose attack feels right is the right answer.

### Is the Shield Lancer's damage enough for Uber bosses?

Uber fights check gear quality and build commitment, not class labels — the whole [roster](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) is clearable on every class with completed builds. Where exactly the Shield Lancer's kill times land in the S10 meta is still settling; verify against the current [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/).

### Where is this class's full talent guide?

In progress, like the other free classes' pages — the expansion is weeks old and patch tuning is active. Publishing talent numbers before they stabilize would just be work to redo; this page completes as the meta settles.
