## The Roster at a Glance

Hero Siege has been adding classes for a decade, and the roster is now deep enough that "which class should I play" needs two answers: what each class **is** (this page), and what each class is **worth right now** (the [Season 10 tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/)). The table below indexes every class with a build guide on this site — twenty-four of them — with the one-line identity the community uses for each.

<Callout type="info" title="A living roster">
Class count and availability shift across seasons — the wiki-era snapshot counted twenty-two, Season 10 expanded the free roster, and the list below grows as each class's build guide is published. Availability details deserve a check on the character select screen.
</Callout>

## Melee and Bruiser Classes

The frontline identities — durability and contact damage as the core promise:

- **[Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/)** — the Whirlwind spin-to-win brawler and the community's default beginner pick.
- **[Butcher](/guides/hero-siege-butcher-build/)** — the sustained-contact bruiser whose campaign leveling is famously smooth.
- **[Shield Lancer](/guides/hero-siege-shield-lancer-build/)** — free since S10; defense as part of the offense.
- **[Marauder](/guides/hero-siege-marauder-build/)** — free since S10; the aggressive momentum pick.
- **[Jötunn](/guides/hero-siege-jotunn-build/)** — free since S10; the deliberate heavyweight.
- **[Samurai](/guides/hero-siege-samurai-build/)** — precise, committed melee windows.
- **[Demon Slayer](/guides/hero-siege-demon-slayer-build/)** — the demon-hunting blade archetype.
- **[Pirate](/guides/hero-siege-pirate-build/)** — the swashbuckling mid-range brawler.

## Classes FAQ

### Why does my class count differ from this page?

Counts drift with seasons — classes get added, availability changes, and the wiki-era twenty-two snapshot predates recent additions. The roster above reflects the classes with published build guides; verify availability in-game.

### Are paid classes stronger than free ones?

No verified evidence sorts power by price — the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) mixes both across its standings, and Season 10's free expansion was read by the community as a statement of confidence in the free picks.

### Which class should a brand-new player pick?

The durable, well-documented melee picks — the [Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) above all — remain the community's beginner default, with the free [Shield Lancer](/guides/hero-siege-shield-lancer-build/) as the defensive alternative. The [beginner guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) covers the first campaign for any of them.
