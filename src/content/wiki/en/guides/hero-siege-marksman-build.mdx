## A Ranged Class Where You Don't Aim

The Gunner Drone Marksman inverts the usual ranged-class contract. Instead of trading shots with enemies, you deploy autonomous drones that acquire targets and fire on their own — your contribution is map movement, threat avoidance, and keeping the swarm alive. The result is a build with the safety of ranged play and the mental load of a much simpler class, which is exactly why it became Season 10's most-documented archetype.

JuxtAPoser's zero-to-hero walkthrough put the build on the map, and endgame pilots like imortilize have shown the same shell deleting Ebontharn's toughest content. Our [Season 10 tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) reflects that consensus: Marksman sits in the top group for both starting and pushing.

## Drone Mechanics: What You Control

Understanding what the drones do — and don't do — is the whole skill ceiling of the build:

- **Drones acquire their own targets** within a radius. Your placement decides *what they can reach*; your movement redraws that circle constantly.
- **They persist between fights**, so opening a new zone with drones already deployed is free damage.
- **Their output scales off your stats and gear**, which is why random drops during leveling already feel strong — every upgrade you equip quietly upgrades the swarm.

The practical consequence: in this build, "good play" means drawing clean walking routes through maps, not mechanical aim. Players who enjoy routing and map movement get the most out of it.

## Talent Priorities

1. **Drone count and upkeep nodes first.** More concurrent drones is the build's main damage multiplier; take the cluster that raises your active drone cap as early as the tree allows.
2. **Drone damage scaling second.** Nodes that let drones inherit more of your offensive stats compound with every gear upgrade you make.
3. **Personal defense third.** You are still a squishy ranged character — a thin band of life and escape-oriented nodes covers the mistakes drones can't absorb for you.
4. **Quality-of-life tail.** Pickup radius, movement, and drone-recall style nodes smooth out the farming loop you will run hundreds of times.

Layer the seasonal systems on top: the [Ether Tree's](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) density branches feed the swarm more targets, which is exactly what it wants.

## Item Scaling: Why the Build Never Falls Off

Most ranged builds have a cliff — a point where random drops stop keeping pace and the build stalls until a specific unique drops. The drone build's scaling curve is unusually smooth:

- **Leveling:** any offensive affix upgrades the drones immediately.
- **Mid-game:** the first real uniques multiply drone output; check the [uniques overview](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) for what the rarity tiers mean.
- **Endgame:** with the full swarm online, the build's ceiling is high enough that endgame showcases run it against Inferno Ubers — the same core that cleared Act 2.

When a specific slot refuses to drop, buy it. The [market guide](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) covers how to shop without overpaying in a live economy.

## Common Mistakes

- **Standing still to "manage" drones.** They manage themselves; your job is movement. Static play gets the body killed while the swarm survives.
- **Overinvesting in personal weapon damage.** The drones inherit your stats, but node selection should favor drone-specific clusters first — generic offense is the slower path.
- **Entering Uber-tier fights without escape tools.** The build's defense is distance; a cornered Marksman is a dead one. Learn each arena's exits before committing — the [Uber bosses guide](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) lists what to expect.

## FAQ

**Drone Marksman or other Marksman paths?**
Gunner Drone is the consensus starter for its scaling curve and documentation depth. The other Marksman directions are viable but demand more from your gear.

**Solo or co-op?**
Both work. In co-op the drones naturally cover area while a melee teammate — a [Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/), say — holds attention.

**What's the single biggest power spike?**
Drone-count nodes. Every additional concurrent drone is effectively a free damage multiplier on the whole build.

**I'm dying in endgame — more damage or more defense?**
Defense, almost always. Drone builds die when the player gets cornered, not when the swarm lacks damage. Re-check your [Incarnation allocations](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) before re-speccing offense.
