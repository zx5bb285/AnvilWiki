## The Question Season 10 Actually Created

[Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) did something quietly significant: it widened the free roster. Four classes — **Marauder**, [Plague Doctor](/guides/hero-siege-plague-doctor-build/), [Shield Lancer](/guides/hero-siege-shield-lancer-build/) and [Jötunn](/guides/hero-siege-jotunn-build/) — joined the free rotation, per community season coverage, and every new player's first question changed from "which class do I buy" to "which free class do I main."

This page is the Marauder's case, argued honestly — including against its three free siblings.

<Callout type="info" title="Verify the free status in-game">
The free-class change is community-reported (Mobalytics' S10 coverage among the sources) rather than confirmed by an official page we can cite. The character select screen is the source of truth; this guide assumes the reporting is right and covers what it means for your season either way.
</Callout>

## The Marauder's Case

Stated plainly: the Marauder is the free roster's aggressive-momentum pick — a class whose community reputation rests on forward pressure rather than caution. For players whose instinct in every ARPG is to close distance and keep swinging, the class description reads like a mirror, and in a [season](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) whose [talent expansion](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) rewards commitment to a build identity, that instinct has room to become a real build.

The honest counterweight: aggressive classes punish learning mistakes harder than forgiving ones. The community's [beginner default](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) remains the durable melee archetypes — which is not a strike against the Marauder, just a framing of who it fits.

The table's honest lesson: at the free tier, the differences are playstyle, not power. All four clear the [campaign](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/) and all four have [S10 tier-list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) defenders. Pick the description that sounds like you.

## Starting a Marauder Season

The progression is the game's standard ramp, with class flavor at each step:

1. **Campaign to level 100** — learn the skill kit by using it. The [beginner guide's](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) leveling mistakes apply to every class.
2. **Climb the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) on momentum.** Aggressive classes farm the [upper ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) well once the build's damage plan is online.
3. **Pick a build identity and commit.** The [talent and Ether systems](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) reward focus; the [league starter page](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) maps the season's proven shells.
4. **Endgame at your own pace** — [wormholes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/), the [Uber roster](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/), and the [farming economy](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) that funds [peak gear](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/).

## Marauder FAQ

### Is the Marauder pay-to-lose compared to paid classes?

No. Hero Siege's class balance does not sort cleanly by price — the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) mixes free and paid classes, and Season 10's free expansion was widely read as confidence in the free roster, not an apology for it.

### Which free class has the most community documentation?

The paid-era veterans — [Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) and [Marksman](/guides/hero-siege-marksman-build/) among them — lead on written material. The four free newcomers are weeks into their S10 documentation; their pages here grow as it settles.

### Should I level a Marauder now or wait for guides?

Now. A level 100 Marauder with real gear is worth more than any guide — the class's own [Ether and talent trees](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) teach its builds faster than reading does, and guides without a character behind them are trivia.
