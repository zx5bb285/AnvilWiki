## The Tips That Every Guide Repeats

Hero Siege's tips-and-tricks genre is large — multiple creators have dedicated videos to it across several seasons, which makes the genre unusually reliable: when four separate guides land on the same lessons, those lessons are load-bearing. This page collects the recurring core, with each tip linked to the system it actually operates in. None of these are secrets; all of them are the difference between a smooth season and a stalled one.

<Callout type="info" title="Structure over numbers">
Specific values shift with 7.0.x patches, so each tip below is stated in its patch-stable form and linked to the page that tracks the numbers.
</Callout>

## Tip 1: Let Kill Speed Tell You When to Climb

The single most-repeated progression tip: the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) moves on kill speed, not on confidence. If elite packs die as fast as trash, the next tier is waiting; if bosses take three times longer than packs, the build needs work first. Players who stall mid-season are almost always farming a comfortable tier for perfect drops instead of pushing an uncomfortable one with a real [build](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/).

## Tip 2: Run One Gold Income From Hour One

Every economy guide converges here: a [gold income](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) running from early in the season — even a small one — compounds into the gear budget that defines the endgame. [Mining](/guides/hero-siege-mining-guide/) is the standard low-variance starter, tower farms take over at endgame clear speeds, and the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) multiplies whatever the first two produce.

## Tip 3: Buy the Gap, Farm the Rest

The market is Hero Siege's time machine: one purchased item in a progression-blocking slot usually costs less than the hours of farming the drop would take. Veterans treat the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) as a standard gear route — check it before grinding, especially early in a season when prices have not settled.

## Tip 4: Start Long Systems Earlier Than Feels Necessary

Relics, the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/), the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) — the game's long systems all reward calendar time more than effort spikes. The [relics guide](/guides/hero-siege-relics-guide/) states it plainly: relics respond to volume, and a relic started late is a relic that is still leveling when the season's real content arrives.

## Tip 5: Gear the Build You Have, Not the Build You Want

Loot discipline's first rule: uniques and drops serve the current build's damage plan, not a hypothetical one. Hoarding mid-tier uniques for a respec you may never run is the classic inventory trap the [beginner guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) warns about — sell, trade or stash by plan.

## Tip 6: Boss Fights Are Mechanic Exams, Not Damage Races

Every boss guide in the community lands on the same framing: pattern knowledge beats raw damage at every tier of the [boss roster](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/). One clean run learning the transitions beats five greedy deaths — and the habit scales all the way to the [Uber layer](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/).

## Tip 7: Patch Day Is Information Day

Balance patches rewrite the meta — which class farms fastest, which build needs a respec, which farm route pays. Veterans read the [patch tracker](/guides/hero-siege-patch-notes/) before the first session after an update, because a stale assumption is the most expensive item in the game.

## Tips FAQ

### Are there tips for party play specifically?

The core set transfers — with one addition from the [co-op and hardcore guide](/guides/hero-siege-hardcore-and-coop-guide/): in groups, your role defines your gear priorities, and durability matters more the closer you fight.

### What about hardcore mode tips?

Hardcore changes the risk calculus on every tip above — the [hardcore guide](/guides/hero-siege-hardcore-and-coop-guide/) covers the survivability-first overrides that one death makes mandatory.

### Which tip do players ignore most?

Tip 3. The instinct to farm rather than buy is deeply ARPG-ingrained, and it is the single largest time sink in an average season — the market exists precisely so that no single slot holds your build hostage.
