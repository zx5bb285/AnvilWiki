## What Is the Ether Tree?

The Ether Tree is the seasonal system that shapes what your runs actually contain: which events spawn, which rewards appear, which risks are worth taking. You earn **Ether** by completing **Ether Quests** — objectives layered into normal gameplay — and spend it to light up nodes on a branching tree. In Season 10 (Ebontharn), the tree grew by **more than 100 new nodes**, which turns it from a side system into one of the season's central build decisions.

Think of it as the opposite of the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/): the Incarnation Tree makes your character stronger; the Ether Tree makes the *world* more rewarding.

<Callout type="info" title="Season 10 numbers move weekly">
The 7.0.x patch series has already buffed several tree nodes (Treasure Chest node chance 1% → 3%, Presence of Evil 1% → 8%, a new Whispering Prophet Ether node added to Chaos Pillars, per the official September patch notes). Verify node values in-game before committing a route.
</Callout>

## The Core Loop: Quests → Ether → Nodes

1. **Run content with Ether Quests active.** Quests ask you to do things you were doing anyway — kill elites, clear events, open containers — with a seasonal objective layered on top.
2. **Bank the Ether.** Completed quests award Ether whether or not the run itself goes well, so quest completion is the priority inside any run.
3. **Spend with intent.** Every node is a promise about how your next hundred runs will look. Spending scattered points produces scattered runs; a filled branch produces a farming strategy.

DonTheCrown's Ebontharn Ether Quest walkthrough and Sleepy Cat's tree overview videos are the community's reference material for the quest loop — well worth a watch if you prefer seeing the flow.

## Early Node Priorities

The community consensus for the first week of a league:

- **Economy nodes first.** Buffed chance nodes (Treasure Chest, Presence of Evil) convert directly into [gold and loot](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/), and their post-patch values make them standout early picks.
- **Density and event nodes second.** More spawns per minute is more of everything — XP, drops, quest progress. These nodes make your [leveling push](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) faster too.
- **Risk nodes last.** Nodes that make runs harder are for when your build can cash them in for better rewards, not while you are still filling your [talent tree](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/).

## Building a Farming Route Around the Tree

"Ether tree strats" — the practice of following a specific branch to create a specific farm — is the system's real endgame. The pattern:

1. **Pick the outcome.** Gold per hour, XP per hour, specific container types, or event density.
2. **Fill the branch that produces that outcome.** Follow one path to completion rather than dabbling; partial branches give partial payoffs.
3. **Match your build to the branch.** A chest-heavy branch rewards a fast clear build; an elite-hunting branch rewards burst single-target. Our [wormhole guide](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) covers build structures that adapt well.
4. **Re-walk the branch after every patch.** Hotfixes have already moved node values this season; a route built on last week's numbers can quietly stop paying.

## Common Mistakes

- **Spreading points thin.** A wide, shallow tree produces nothing measurable. Depth first, breadth later.
- **Ignoring Ether Quests while grinding.** The quests are the currency source; skipping them starves the whole system.
- **Trusting stale node values.** Season 10 is being tuned live — this page included. Check the official notes when a route stops performing.

## FAQ

**Where do I see my Ether Tree?**
In the seasonal menu from the main hub. The tree view shows claimed, claimable, and locked nodes along their branch paths.

**Is the Ether Tree per character?**
It follows the seasonal character structure — progress is tied to your seasonal play. Confirm the exact persistence rules in the official season documentation.

**What is the single best node?**
There is no permanent answer while hotfixes keep landing — but buffed economy nodes have been the standout value of early Season 10.

**Do Ether Quests work in co-op?**
Yes, and co-op groups commonly split quest objectives to clear them faster.
