## What Is Hero Siege, and What Are You Getting Into?

Hero Siege is a fast-paced hack-and-slash action RPG with roguelike and dungeon-crawling elements, developed by Panic Art Studio. You battle waves of demons across the world of Tarethiel, loot gear of escalating rarity, and build a character around deep skill trees. You can play solo or in online and local co-op, and the game is structured around **seasonal leagues** — roughly comparable to seasons in Diablo or Path of Exile — that refresh the meta and add new systems.

The single most important thing to understand as a beginner: **your first character is a learning character, not a trophy.** The campaign teaches you the game; the endgame is where the real loop lives. Do not agonize over your first class pick.

## Which Class Should a Beginner Pick?

Hero Siege offers many classes, each with its own skill tree and playstyles, and Season 10's patch reworked several of them. The honest beginner advice:

1. **Pick a melee or straightforward ranged class first.** Melee classes like the Viking forgive positioning mistakes because they are durable; simple projectile classes let you learn bullet patterns from a safe distance.
2. **Follow the leveling flow, not a "best" tier list.** Tier lists rank optimized endgame power, not beginner-friendliness. Our [Season 10 class tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) explains the current meta, and our [league starter builds](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) highlight classes that are both strong and easy to pilot.
3. **Do not buy DLC classes for your first run.** The base roster is more than enough to learn every system.

## The First Playthrough: Campaign to Level 100

Your first hours follow the story acts. As of Season 10 there are **nine acts**, each with its own zone theme, enemy sets, and a boss fight at the end. The campaign exists to teach you the core verbs of the game: dodge, kite, pile on damage, and loot everything.

Practical rules for the campaign:

- **Talk to every NPC and do side quests as you go.** Quests unlock systems, stash space, and progression options you will want later.
- **Loot and equip freely while leveling.** Gear has random affixes; while leveling, almost any upgrade is a real upgrade. The nuance of [unique items and rune words](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) matters at endgame, not at level 20.
- **Learn your dodge and movement tools early.** Hero Siege is far more survivable when you treat movement as a skill, not an afterthought.
- **Push into each difficulty when the current one stops challenging you.** The game's difficulty ladder (Normal, Nightmare, Hell, Inferno) is where character power is truly tested.

## What Are Seasons, and Should You Care?

Each season is a fresh competitive league with a new theme, balance changes, and often a new progression system. Season 9 introduced the massive Incarnation passive tree; Season 10 added Act 9, new Uber bosses, and a bigger Ether Tree. Seasons are the reason much of the internet's Hero Siege advice is stale — a guide from Season 5 may describe mechanics that no longer exist.

For a beginner, the practical takeaway: **start your first real character in the current season.** Seasonal characters play together, the economy is freshest, and all the current guides — including ours — apply to you. Our [Season 10 guide](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) covers what this season specifically changes.

## Your First Week: a Simple Plan

1. **Days 1–2:** Clear the campaign on Normal. Do not skip cutscenes you care about, but do not grind either — the campaign is a corridor, not a sandbox.
2. **Days 2–4:** Push difficulties with your campaign character. Follow our [leveling and progression route](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) to keep damage competitive.
3. **Days 4–7:** Hit level 100, unlock endgame systems, and start your first [wormhole runs](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) — infinitely scaling dungeons that are the heart of endgame.
4. **Along the way:** Start earning gold with safe [early farming methods](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) so you can afford the market's starter gear.

## Mistakes Every Beginner Makes (and How to Skip Them)

- **Hoarding low-tier gear.** Vendor or break down everything that is not a clear upgrade; inventory space is a resource.
- **Ignoring the seasonal system.** Seasonal currencies and trees (like the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/)) are where most of your power comes from each league.
- **Spending gold early.** You will want it at level 100. See our [gold and market guide](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) before your first big purchase.
- **Reading outdated wikis.** Much of the existing Hero Siege documentation stopped updating years ago. Check the version a guide targets before trusting its numbers.

## FAQ

**Is Hero Siege pay-to-win or does it need DLC?**
No. The base game contains the full campaign and endgame. DLC adds extra classes and cosmetics-style content, not required power.

**Is there multiplayer?**
Yes — online co-op and local co-op are both supported.

**How long is the campaign?**
Most players reach the end of the acts and level 100 within their first week of casual play. Endgame progression is effectively endless.

**Do I need to know the lore?**
No. The story gives structure to the nine acts, but all systems are learnable without it.

**Where do I redeem gift codes?**
In-game: open chat and type `#` to open the redeem window. See our [codes page](/codes/all-codes/) for known codes and history.
