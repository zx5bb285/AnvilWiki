## Where the Notes Actually Live

Hero Siege's patch pipeline has two layers, and knowing the difference saves wasted reading: the **official news section** on the developer's site carries the current 7.0.x releases, and **Steam announcements** mirror the significant ones. Legacy patch portals from earlier eras still rank in search results — they stopped updating before [Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) and are history references only.

## What the 7.0.x Line Has Changed

The Ebontharn tuning line so far, from the documented releases:

- **Drop-rate bumps** — chest-node drop rates moved from 1% to 3% in the 7.0.11 line, a direct buff to farming routes everywhere.
- **Ether system additions** — new Ether nodes appeared on Chaos Pillars, touching the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) economy and everything fed by it.
- **Class hotfixes** — named-ability fixes have shipped silently between numbered releases; class players should check after every hotfix, not just the big notes.
- **Presence tuning** — aura-adjacent values received multi-point adjustments in the same window.

Each of those is small in isolation and compounding in practice — which is exactly why a tracker page exists.

## Why Every Patch Is a Decision Point

In a season-based ARPG, patch notes are not reading material — they are inputs to three standing decisions:

1. **Does my build still hold its tier?** Balance changes move the [class standings](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/); a committed build's next session should start with the diff.
2. **Is my farm route still optimal?** Drop-rate and reward changes reorder the [gold farming](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) menu — a route that printed gold last week may be obsolete, and a buffed one may be waiting.
3. **Is now the time to start something long?** System additions — like the Ether node additions above — change the value of starting [long systems](/guides/hero-siege-relics-guide/) early.

## How to Read a Hero Siege Patch Note

The community's patch-reading habit, compressed:

1. **Search your class's name first.** Named-ability changes are the personal news; everything else is context.
2. **Read the numbers, not the adjectives.** "Improved" means nothing without the before/after — the official notes carry the values.
3. **Check the silent hotfixes.** Between numbered releases, the developer ships unannounced fixes; community videos usually catch them within a day.
4. **Update one thing, not everything.** The disciplined response to a patch is one build adjustment or one farm-route change — not a full respec every two weeks.

## Patch Notes FAQ

### Do patches reset my character or season progress?

Balance patches tune values; they do not reset characters. Season boundaries are the reset points — and how seasons roll over is a season-structure question, not a patch question.

### Where do I report something that looks like a bug from a patch?

The developer's official support channels carry bug reports — the community hub mirrors them. A wiki documents what changed; it cannot fix it.

### Why do patch changes matter for a wiki?

Because most wiki content is numbers in disguise. Every build guide, [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) and [farm route](/guides/hero-siege-chaos-tower-guide/) on this site carries `gameVersion` markers and patch-linked refresh points — the tracker page is the index of when those refreshes happened.
