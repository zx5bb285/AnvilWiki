## The Free Class That Rewards Experience

[Season 10's](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) free-class expansion added four classes to the no-purchase roster — [Marauder](/guides/hero-siege-marauder-build/), **Plague Doctor**, [Shield Lancer](/guides/hero-siege-shield-lancer-build/) and [Jötunn](/guides/hero-siege-jotunn-build/) — and the Plague Doctor is the one whose value scales with how much Hero Siege you already know.

The reasoning is playstyle-shaped: affliction-flavored damage asks more of its player than swing-and-survive melee does. It rewards knowing where packs spawn, how [act bosses](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/) phase, and which [endgame systems](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pay for a second character's gear — knowledge a first-time player is still earning. As an alt, the class plays to everything you already learned.

## Why Alts Matter in a Season Model

Hero Siege is a seasonal game, and a season's economy has a shape: the [main character](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) builds the [gold income](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) and the [farming routine](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/), then everything after inherits it. A second character skips the hardest phase of the game — the broke-and-leveling phase — because:

- **The market funds it.** Your main's [market economy](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) knowledge and gold reserve buy an alt's mid-tier gear directly.
- **The ladder is already mapped.** You know the [difficulty progression](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) — an alt climbs it in half the sessions.
- **Knowledge transfers, muscle memory does not.** An affliction caster rebuilds your instincts from zero, which is the actual entertainment you are buying.

## Starting a Plague Doctor Alt

1. **Set the alt's budget first.** Decide what your main's [gold reserve](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) can spend on the alt's transition gear — a number, not a vibe. The [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) fills slots while the alt levels.
2. **Campaign with the endgame in mind.** Read the [beginner guide's](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) mistakes list from the other side — an alt skips them because you already made them.
3. **Commit to one build identity by mid-campaign.** The [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) and [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) compound whatever you feed them; feeding them a decision beats feeding them curiosity.
4. **Enter endgame through the alt-cheapest door.** [Wormhole farming](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) and [Uber volume](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/) run fine on second-character gear, and they produce the [top-tier drops](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) that make the alt self-sufficient.

## Plague Doctor FAQ

### Is the Plague Doctor good, actually?

The honest S10 answer: the free roster's classes are all viable, and "good" at this stage of the season is documented by [tier-list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) consensus rather than settled numbers. What the class verifiably offers is a distinct playstyle at zero purchase cost — the rest is a developing meta.

### Should a brand-new player start here instead?

Probably not — the class rewards game knowledge a new player does not have yet. The free roster's steadier picks ([Shield Lancer](/guides/hero-siege-shield-lancer-build/), or the [Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) standard) teach the game with more forgiveness, and Plague Doctor is still free when you are ready.

When the class's S10 documentation stabilizes — talent routes shift with patch tuning, and publishing numbers before they settle is how wikis earn corrections. This page grows into the full breakdown as the meta firms up.
