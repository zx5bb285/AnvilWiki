## What Is the Codex of Eternity?

The Codex of Eternity is Hero Siege's answer to a loot-game classic problem: random drops rarely give you exactly what your build needs. Instead of leaving every slot to chance, the Codex gives you a crafting framework for steering gear toward specific affixes — and in a season like [Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/), where endgame builds hinge on a handful of key stats, that control is worth more than raw farming hours.

<Callout type="info" title="Community-documented, not official">
Hero Siege has no official crafting documentation, so details below reflect what guides and top players currently practice. Exact material costs and rates shift with patches — verify in-game before committing a stockpile.
</Callout>

## How the Codex Works

The short version, as practiced by the community:

1. **Collect materials** from endgame farming — [wormhole pushes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/), Uber kills and high-difficulty runs are the usual sources.
2. **Choose a base item** whose item type fits your build's weapon or armor needs.
3. **Craft or re-roll affixes** toward the stats your build scales with, instead of accepting whatever dropped.
4. **Iterate.** The Codex is a loop, not a slot machine you pull once — guides consistently frame it as "print, check, repeat."

The reason this matters for [rarity chasing](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) is that drop-based farming gives you no control over which base item rolls. The Codex lets you aim effort at a specific slot — the difference between "someday an Angelic might drop" and "I am working toward an Angelic weapon."

## Why Top Players "Print" Angelics in the Codex

The strongest endgame items in Hero Siege sit in the [Angelic and Unholy tiers](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/), and the community treats a first Angelic as a season milestone. Guides from top players consistently land on the same thesis: farming Angles purely from drops is a lottery, while the Codex converts materials into targeted attempts — a process players describe as "printing" Angelics.

That framing comes with a warning worth repeating: printing is a **rate improvement, not a promise**. Budget your materials, expect junk results between good ones, and stop-loss when a slot has consumed more than you planned.

## Common Codex Mistakes

- **Crafting before your build is settled.** Every re-roll spent toward affixes you will replace next week is wasted. Lock your build direction first — the [class build guides](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) are the baseline.
- **Ignoring material income.** Crafting without a farming loop behind it drains your stockpile in a session. Pair Codex sessions with [gold farming](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) and endgame runs.
- **Chasing perfect rolls too early.** A good-enough craft that unblocks progression beats weeks of chasing a perfect one — you can revisit the slot in the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) or with more materials later.

## Codex FAQ

### Does the Codex replace farming?

No — it feeds on it. Every craft consumes materials that come from playing the game: drops, gold, endgame kills. The Codex converts play time into targeted gear progress; it does not skip it.

### Which items should I craft first?

The slot that most limits your build — usually the weapon, since damage scaling touches every other system. Armor and jewelry slots come after your damage base is stable.

### Is the Codex different in Season 10?

The system carried into Season 10 with the [Ebontharn update](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/), and the community's crafting routes still apply. Exact numbers may have been touched by 7.0.x patches, so re-verify costs in-game before large sessions.
