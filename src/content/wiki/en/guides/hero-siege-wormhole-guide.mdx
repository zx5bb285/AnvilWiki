## What Wormholes Are

Wormholes are Hero Siege's infinitely scaling endgame dungeons — the system the entire loot chase funnels into. Each wormhole level raises enemy power and reward quality, and your season is, in large part, a question of how deep you can push before your build hits its ceiling.

The community treats wormhole depth the way other ARPGs treat greater rifts: it is the measuring stick for build power, the venue for the best drops, and the reason optimization matters. Players have published clears at depths above 100 in recent seasons — wormhole 137 showcases exist — but those are outliers with hyper-optimized gear. Most players' meaningful progression happens far below the records.

## The Pushing Loop

A pushing session looks like this:

1. **Enter at your current comfortable depth.** Not your record — your comfortable clear. Depth you can clear quickly earns more per hour than depth you barely survive.
2. **Clear fast, kill the guardian.** Each wormhole level ends with a boss-tier fight. Guardian kills are the checkpoint that matters; skipping them to race depth wastes the run.
3. **Step up in small increments.** Two or three levels at a time. When a level takes noticeably longer or kills start sticking, that is your current ceiling — farm one or two levels below it.
4. **Bank loot, fix gaps, repeat.** Every push session should end with an inventory check: what piece of gear, talent node, or [Ether Tree branch](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) was the actual bottleneck?

## What a Pushing Build Needs

Class matters less than structure. Whatever you play — Viking, Marksman, Pyromancer, Bard — a pushing build converges on the same four properties:

- **Mobility.** Time-per-level is the economy of pushing. Builds that cross rooms fast out-earn marginally stronger but slower ones.
- **Sustain.** Enemy damage scales until chip damage is constant. Some form of self-healing or mitigation is what turns level 60 from a wall into a speed bump.
- **Single-target burst.** Guardians are the gate on depth. Area damage clears the map; burst kills the boss before its enrage-style mechanics stack up.
- **Consistent scaling from your systems.** Your [talent build](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/), [Incarnation nodes](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/), and item set must all compound — a pushing build is a system, not a weapon.

## Realistic Depth Goals for a Season

A sensible first-season ladder, based on how the community's seasons typically flow:

| Phase | Goal |
|---|---|
| Fresh 100 | Unlock wormholes, clear the opening depths comfortably |
| First week | Push past the early tiers with a complete starter build |
| Mid-season | Deep enough that guardians resist — usually where [gold farming](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) via wormholes starts competing with dedicated farms |
| End of season | Personal record depth, plus your first serious [unique finds](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) |

Exact level numbers vary wildly by class, gear, and patch — anyone quoting a single "should reach level X" number is guessing. Use the community's published showcases as orientation, not as quotas.

## Common Mistakes

- **Pushing on a farming build.** Clear-speed builds and guardian-killing builds overlap less than you would think; carry a target-loadout mindset.
- **Chasing record depth every session.** Farming one or two levels below your ceiling is faster per loot earned, and faster loot is what raises your ceiling.
- **Ignoring defensive nodes.** At depth, deaths cost more than they ever did earlier — the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) defensive spine pays for itself here.
- **Comparing your week 2 to someone's month 6.** Endgame showcases are aspirational content, not baselines.

## FAQ

**Are wormholes different in Season 10?**
The system is stable across seasons; what changes is the meta around it — new nodes, class reworks, and balance. Season 10's talent expansion in particular reshaped pushing builds.

**Solo or co-op for pushing?**
Both work. Co-op scales enemies but splits aggro and adds build synergy; solo is simpler to optimize. Community record pushes exist in both formats.

**Do wormholes drop the best items in the game?**
They are a top source of high-tier loot, but Uber boss fights and crafted/rerolled gear compete. A balanced endgame uses all three.

**What is the "cheese" I see in pushing videos?**
Strategies that bypass intended difficulty — terrain abuse, AI quirks, specific interactions. They are patched irregularly; don't build your season around one.
