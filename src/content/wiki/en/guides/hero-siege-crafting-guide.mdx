## What Can the Act 1 Cube Do?

Every item that matters in Hero Siege eventually sits inside one box. The Crafting Cube — handed over in Act 1 and never replaced — is the engine room of the item economy: drops and gold are the fuel, but the cube is where value actually gets manufactured. Five functions run inside it:

1. **Rerolling affixes** — new stat lines on an item you want to keep. This is the function with teeth; see the risk section below.
2. **Adding sockets** — turning a clean piece into a socketed one.
3. **Removing sockets** — via the Blacksmith's Mallet, covered below.
4. **Star upgrades** — pushing an item up its star track with dust.
5. **Purification and cloning** — cleaning corruption and duplicating items.

<Callout type="info" title="The recipe book is in the game">
Every formula the cube uses — including the tarot-card recipes — is listed in the in-game **Journal** menu. When a community guide and your Journal disagree, trust the Journal; recipes are exactly the kind of detail that drifts between patches.
</Callout>

The cube also has a separate, order-sensitive life as the rune-word machine. That system has its own four-gate rulebook on the [rune words page](/items/hero-siege-runewords/), which this page deliberately does not repeat.

## What Does a Failed Reroll Cost?

Rerolling is the cube's highest-value function and its only one that can hurt you. A failed reroll **corrupts the item: stats are deducted**, and the deduction stays until you purify. The damage is bounded — a corrupted piece remains intact enough to sell — but the stat loss can quietly ruin an item you spent a season's budget on.

The repair path: **Prophet's Wisdom** or **Angel's Wisdom** purifies a corrupted item. Which produces the prudent sequence for every reroll project: decide *before* rolling whether you own — or can afford — a purifier, because a reroll is not truly finished until its failure case is paid for.

## How Do the Dust Tiers Work?

Dust is the cube's fuel for star upgrades, and it comes in three families, each sourced by decomposing gear of its own quality:

| Dust | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Angelic Dust | Decomposing Angelic and Unholy pieces |
| Mythic Dust | Decomposing Mythic pieces |
| Satanic Dust | Decomposing Satanic pieces — graded D through SS |

The rule that makes the system readable: **each dust tier only upgrades items of that same tier.** A star upgrade is the item plus same-tier dust in the cube, and nothing else moves it. This is also what makes decomposing rational at the top end: a [Satanic](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) piece you will never wear is worth more as dust funding the piece you will.

## What Else Lives in the Cube?

- **The Blacksmith's Mallet removes sockets.** You will rarely hold one by accident: twenty Mallet Fragments combine into a single Mallet, and the fragment recipe itself consumes tarot cards — 20 Tower and 10 Star by community documentation. Confirm the exact numbers in your Journal before spending cards.
- **Gems merge upward.** Three gems of the same tier become one gem of the next. Save the merges for when the higher tier is actually the bottleneck of a project.
- **Runes merge too.** Three lower runes combine into one higher rune, which is one half of the two-way rune economy — the pricing quirks on the rune side are their own subject on the rune words page.

## Where Does Crafting Meet the Market?

The cube and the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) are one loop, not two systems. Crafters buy the bases and materials farmers sell; a rerolled or star-upgraded item re-enters the market with its new stat lines priced in; and even a corrupted item is market-legal, which turns failed rerolls into a discount niche rather than pure trash. On the materials side, decomposition and [mining](/guides/hero-siege-mining-guide/) feed the same economy from below.

The cube is also not the ceiling of gear-side power. The augment system runs on its own [Angelic Key economy](/items/hero-siege-augments-guide/) — one step beyond what dust can reach.

## Crafting FAQ

### Which dust should I save and which should I spend?

Spend the tier your current project needs and bank the rest — tiers do not convert into each other, so a stack of the wrong dust is dead weight for the item you are actually upgrading. Climbing tiers mid-season? Decompose off-tier gear to keep every bank topped up.

### Is crafting better than farming for gear?

They answer different questions. Farming decides which items you own; crafting decides what those items become. The community pattern is to farm the base and then craft the result — which is why the two loops share one economy instead of competing.

### What should I do before my first big reroll?

Price the failure, not the success. Confirm you hold a purifier — Prophet's Wisdom or Angel's Wisdom — or the budget to buy one, read the recipe in the Journal rather than from memory, and make sure the item is worth sitting corrupted while you gather the fix.
