## What Actually Pays Out Experience?

Hero Siege pays experience for exactly one thing: kills per minute. The clock does not matter, the zone's scenery does not matter, and neither does how dangerous the content looks — a character that clears a weaker area twice as fast out-levels one grinding a harder area at half the pace. Every habit below exists to push one number up: how many enemies die per minute while you are actually playing.

That framing also explains the most common leveling mistake. Players stall because they stop moving — backtracking for a chest, clearing every side room, standing still through a pack's death animation. The community's summary is blunt: buy movement boots early, because movement speed is experience.

## How Do You Keep the Kill Rate Up?

Tempo has a shelf life. While your character one-shots a zone, stay there and keep the pace flat — keep moving, never camp, let packs die as you pass through them. The moment kills stop being instant, the efficient move is not persistence. It is maintenance.

The community pattern for the pre-70 stretch: when one-shotting dries up, take your gear to the blacksmith and redo it with dust, rather than grinding a dead zone for drops that will not come. Where that dust comes from is the next section — and the [crafting guide](/guides/hero-siege-crafting-guide/) covers the forge side in full.

One number is worth knowing, with a warning attached: enemies roughly **±20 levels** away from your character reportedly pay little or no experience. That threshold is **community-documented and patch-sensitive** — it has moved before and will move again — so treat it as a signal that a zone is dead for you, not as a guarantee you can plan decimals around.

## Why Does Your Next Character Start in the Stash?

Alt leveling is where Hero Siege's systems compound. Dust — the legendary and mythic powder used to reroll and upgrade gear — moves through the stash: park a high-level character's spare powder there, and a fresh character can forge usable equipment from level one instead of hoping drops cooperate.

This is why experienced players treat leveling as an account project, not a per-character project. The first character climbs slowly because it farms its own dust; every character after it inherits the bank. If you plan to try a second class this season — the [class tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) is one way to pick — banking powder first is the cheapest head start available.

## Where Do the Reset Loops Run?

Community leveling routes converge on the same trick: clear a short, dense stretch, reset the area, repeat. The zones most often named:

- **Fields of Battle (Act 1)** — the early loop: dense, flat, fast.
- **Pyramid L1–L2 (Act 3)** — tight corridors and heavy pack density.
- **The Depths of Hell (Act 6)** — the classic pre-endgame experience floor.

Two additions make the loop pay better. First, activate waypoints as you pass through the acts — a reset loop is only as cheap as its re-entry. Second, detour into **Unstable Rifts** and **Chaos Towers** when you encounter them; community guidance treats both as unusually experience-dense content for their time cost.

## Do Relics and the Difficulty Ladder Change the Math?

Both do, and they compound. On the relic side, the collection includes movement- and talent-flavored relics that directly accelerate leveling — flight and speed effects shorten every reset loop you run. The [relics guide](/guides/hero-siege-relics-guide/) covers how a collection grows and which upgrades matter.

On the ladder side, leveling is not farming one tier forever. The standard community route: push through Normal and Nightmare, open Hell, farm there until the build is honest, then push [Inferno](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/), because higher tiers pay more for the same kills. The boss gates along that route map onto the campaign's nine acts — the [act bosses guide](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/) tracks the walls.

## Leveling FAQ

### Does difficulty affect leveling speed?

Yes — higher ladder tiers pay more per kill, which is why the community route pushes to Hell and farms there instead of staying comfortable in Nightmare. Exact per-tier values are not officially published, so treat specific multipliers you read as community estimates.

### Should I share gear between characters?

Share materials, not just gear. Dust and powders sitting in the stash are the real hand-me-down: a fresh character that can forge its own equipment at level one outpaces one waiting for drops. That is the entire alt strategy in one sentence.

### Why did my leveling suddenly slow down?

Usually one of two things: your one-shots stopped (fix: a forge pass with banked dust), or you out-leveled your farming zone's experience window (fix: move up the ladder or switch to a denser reset loop). The [beginner guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) collects the other classic stalling habits.
