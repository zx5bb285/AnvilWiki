## Why Mining Deserves a Spot in Your Routine

Most Hero Siege farming guides start with combat: packs, bosses, drops. Mining is the quiet alternative — a harvesting loop whose income scales with where you do it rather than how lucky your drops are. In a season whose defining economic change is a [reworked market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/), predictable gold income has never been more directly convertible into gear progress, and mining is the most low-variance source of it in the game.

<Callout type="info" title="Numbers are community-reported">
Exact gold values per node, per hour or per difficulty are not officially documented, and they shift with patches and market prices. The figures quoted in mining videos are snapshots — treat them as proof of magnitude, not as rates you are owed.
</Callout>

## How Mining Works

The loop is simple: mining nodes spawn across the game's zones, you harvest them as you pass, and the ore converts into gold. The strategic question is never "how" — it is **where**. Node output scales with difficulty, which is why the community's serious mining guides do not farm it in Normal.

That scaling makes mining a natural companion to the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/): every tier you climb raises the floor under your mining income without any extra effort on your part.

## Where to Mine: the Difficulty Question

Community guides converge on the higher-middle difficulties as the sweet spot — content you can clear fast enough to keep the route moving, with output that actually justifies the trip. The most-watched S10 mining guide puts it in its title: **millions from mining in Nightmare**, and the route habits it demonstrates travel up the ladder as your build improves.

## Fitting Mining into a Farming Week

Mining works best as a **filler loop**, not a main activity. The pattern that shows up across guides:

1. **Route mining between combat blocks.** Harvest nodes along the way whenever you are already traveling through high-difficulty zones — the gold accrues without costing dedicated time.
2. **Dedicated mining sessions when saving for a purchase.** When a [market item](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) is in sight, an hour of routed mining closes the gap faster than hoping for a drop to sell.
3. **Re-evaluate after each difficulty jump.** Your mining route's value changes every time your build moves up a tier — a route that printed gold in Hell may be obsolete in Inferno.

## How Mining Compares to Other Gold Sources

Hero Siege's [gold farming](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) menu has three main entries: combat farming, mining and the endgame money loops around [wormholes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) and Ubers. Their trade-offs:

- **Combat farming** — highest ceiling, drop-luck dependent.
- **Mining** — lowest variance, scales with difficulty, no gear requirements.
- **Endgame loops** — best once your build is complete; punishing before that.

The honest advice: they are not competitors. Mining covers the gaps while combat farming searches for the spike — and Season 10's economy rewards players who run both.

## Mining FAQ

### Does mining require a specific character or build?

No. Mining routes run on any build that can survive the zone and clear packs quickly — mobility and kill speed are the only real requirements, which is why [fast clearing builds](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) mine more per hour.

### Is mining better in a group?

Community opinions differ on group versus solo mining efficiency, and neither has authoritative numbers. Solo routing is the default in guides because it keeps the whole loop under your control.

### Did Season 10 change mining?

The S10 economy rework did not target mining directly, but it raised the value of everything gold can buy — which is exactly why mining guides multiplied after Ebontharn launched. Verify current node behavior in-game as 7.0.x patches continue.
