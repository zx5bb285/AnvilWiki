## A New Market for a New Season

Hero Siege's player market has existed for years, but Season 10 rebuilt it. The developer shipped a dedicated "Reworked Marketplace" video at launch, and the community reaction — from Sleepy Cat's first-impressions breakdown to JuxtAPoser's enthusiastic market update review — has been consistently positive: browsing, listing, and pricing all work noticeably better than the system most older guides describe.

That last point matters more than it sounds: **most market advice on the internet predates this rework.** If a guide talks about the market as an afterthought, it is describing the old one.

<Callout type="info" title="Trading changed the season's rhythm">
With a functioning market, Season 10's optimal loop is farm liquidity → buy upgrades → farm faster. Players who ignore trading and rely purely on self-found drops are, in practice, playing a slower season.
</Callout>

## How the Reworked Market Works

The reworked market follows the conventions modern ARPG traders expect:

- **Listings with search and filters.** You can browse what other players have posted and filter by item type and properties rather than paging through noise.
- **Gold as the medium.** Everything trades for gold, which makes the [gold farming guide](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) the supply side of every purchase you will ever make.
- **Prices move with the season.** Week 1 prices are scarcity prices; week 4 prices reflect supply. The same item can halve in cost as more players reach endgame.

For a sense of what the community values and why, pair this page with the [uniques overview](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) — knowing what an item does is the foundation of knowing what it should cost.

## Buying: Get the Upgrade Without Getting Bled

1. **Name the gap before opening the market.** "I need a better weapon" loses money; "I need X-type weapon with attack speed" finds it. If you cannot name the gap, farm more and buy later.
2. **Price the slot, not the item.** Check several listings for comparable items first. The first acceptable listing is rarely the market price.
3. **Buy in the middle of the season, not the start.** Early-season prices carry a novelty premium. Unless the item unblocks your entire progression, waiting a week is often a 30-50% discount — wait out scarcity when you can.
4. **Budget from your farming rate.** If your best farm earns G gold per hour, an upgrade is worth roughly the hours it saves. Keep that arithmetic honest.

## Selling: Turn Surplus Into Progress

- **List fast, price honest.** Mid-tier gear with good rolls is the market's bread and butter — endgame players gap-filling. Price against current comparable listings, not against your hopes.
- **Sell the early weeks.** Items that feel mediocre to you are exactly what the wave of players behind you needs. Week-2 supply shortages favor sellers.
- **Re-price stale listings.** The market moves daily in a live season; a listing from last week is quoting last week's economy.

## Safe-Trading Habits

- **Use the market interface, not handshake deals.** The reworked system exists so that trades are recorded and safe; off-system arrangements are where scams live.
- **Be suspicious of urgency.** "Quick, before I cancel" pressure is the oldest scam pattern in trading games. The market has no closing bell.
- **Verify item details on the listing, not in chat.** Compare what is listed with what arrives — mismatches are rare in the interface but common in informal deals.

## FAQ

**Do I need to be a high level to use the market?**
No — but most useful listings target endgame builds. Early-game, the market is most valuable for selling your surplus rather than buying.

**What happened to the Tarethiel Market?**
The classic market hub evolved into the reworked system; community content from the season's launch week covers the transition if you knew the old interface.

**Can I trade with friends directly?**
The market is the supported route for item exchange. Direct handoff mechanics have varied across patches — test in-game before planning around it.

**Where does the gold for market shopping come from?**
From farming — see the [gold farming guide](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) for methods ranked by season phase, and the [Season 10 overview](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) for how the reworked market fits the season's other changes.

**Is the market available on console and mobile?**
Trading follows the game across platforms, but interface details differ; check the platform's current build. Season 9 and 10 updates shipped for Steam and PS5, with other platforms following the release cadence.
