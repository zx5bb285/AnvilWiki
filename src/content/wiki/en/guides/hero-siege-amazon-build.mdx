## The Class With the Longest Memory

Hero Siege has been adding classes for years — the roster now runs past twenty names — but the Amazon was there near the start, and its official trailer media goes back far enough to document the game's own visual evolution. Watching the class's promotional history is a tour of the whole game getting older: art, UI and effects changing around a playstyle idea that never needed to change — [ranged](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) damage, fought on the archer's terms.

That history is worth something in a [season](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) that just added a [free-class layer](/guides/hero-siege-marauder-build/) and [three new Ubers](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/): the Amazon is the pick whose identity is the most settled — you know what the class is before you press play.

## What the Archetype Offers in S10

The ranged bargain is as old as ARPGs: trade the safety margin of distance for the responsibility of aiming. In Hero Siege's current endgame — [pack-dense Inferno](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/), [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) timers, [Uber burst windows](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) — that bargain plays out as mobility discipline: ranged classes farm well when their players keep fights happening at their own range, and the [tier list's](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) ranged entries live or die by how honestly their players do that math.

## How a Modern Amazon Season Starts

The progression is the game's proven arc:

1. **Campaign at range** — learn the kit's rhythm on the standard [leveling route](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/), one kit committed instead of five sampled.
2. **Climb the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) on positioning, not gear.** Ranged classes can farm above their gear tier for longer than melee — use that margin.
3. **Invest the long systems in one damage plan** — the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) and [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) compound commitment.
4. **Enter the endgame economy** — [league starter](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) routines, [wormhole volume](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/), the [Uber rotation](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/), and the [market-and-gold loop](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) that fills [gear slots](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) between drops.

## Amazon FAQ

### Amazon or Marksman for ranged players?

The [Marksman](/guides/hero-siege-marksman-build/) carries the community's deepest documented endgame material right now — the [drone build](/items/hero-siege-marksman-bis/) in particular. The Amazon's case is the archetype itself: classic bow-distance damage with the game's longest identity history. Try both; the documentation gap is real but temporary.

### Is the Amazon's trailer-era playstyle still what it plays like today?

Classes evolve across years of patches — talent reworks and season changes touch every kit, the Amazon's included. The trailer documents heritage; the current season's community videos document reality. Use both, weighted by age.

### Does the class have endgame proof like the Bard's Inferno run?

Community endgame footage exists across the roster, and class-specific demonstrations land on this page as they are verified.
