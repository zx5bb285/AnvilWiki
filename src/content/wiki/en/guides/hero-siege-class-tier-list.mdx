## How This Tier List Was Made (and Why Most Are Already Stale)

Season 10 launched on August 21, 2026 with class reworks, a wave of buffs and nerfs, and a talent tree expansion of over 600 nodes. Any tier list written before that patch — including most content still ranking on search engines, and the official wiki's class pages, which predate several seasons — describes a game that no longer exists.

This page aggregates what dedicated Season 10 creators (Graxy_TV, ToumaTv, MinMaxedARPG and others) published after launch, cross-checked against the 7.0.x patch notes. It is a **community-consensus read, not a tested spreadsheet** — treat it as a map of the meta, not gospel.

## The Season 10 Meta at a Glance

Rather than a fake-precise S/A/B/C table, here is how the community's Season 10 consensus actually breaks down:

**Repeatedly rated top-tier for both starting and pushing:**

- **Viking (Whirlwind)** — durable, simple, and the community's default recommendation for a first character of the season. Graxy_TV's starter-and-progression guide and DonTheCrown's week-one series both built around it.
- **Marksman (Gunner Drone builds)** — the drone playstyle scales strongly into endgame; JuxtAPoser's zero-to-hero guide and multiple endgame showcases feature it as one of the season's standout builds.
- **Pyromancer** — Pohx's Season 10 build guide and general community sentiment place it among the smoothest casters this season.

**Strong but more execution-dependent:** Bard (praised by Jcob for clearing all nine acts' bosses on Inferno, but demanding), Amazon (lightning builds with a long track record in wormhole pushes), Butcher (excellent clearing, distinctive playstyle that not everyone enjoys).

**Perfectly playable, consensus mid:** the remainder of the roster. "Mid" in a tier list does not mean weak — it means either slower league starts or builds that need specific items to come online.

## How to Actually Choose Your Class

1. **New to the game?** Pick from the top group above, or see our [league starter builds](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) — they are filtered for beginner-friendliness first, power second.
2. **Returning player with a main?** Play your main. The talent expansion changed everyone; re-learning your class is cheaper than re-learning the game.
3. **Chasing wormhole records?** Read the reasoning, not the rank: pushing builds need mobility, sustain, and single-target for guardian fights. Our [wormhole guide](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) explains what a pushing build needs structurally.
4. **Bossing focus?** Uber fights reward survivability and burst windows — see the [Uber bosses guide](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) before committing.

## Why Tier Lists Distort More Than They Inform

Hero Siege is a loot-and-crafting game before it is a class game. Within any class, the gap between a lazy build and an optimized one dwarfs the gap between adjacent tiers. Community reviewers themselves keep making this point — the game's negative Steam reviews that cite build shallowness are usually describing unoptimized play, while optimized builds of almost any class trivialize mid-tier content.

A class is three things in Season 10: its reworked talent tree, its item dependencies, and your enjoyment of its rhythm. A tier list can only see the first.

## FAQ

**Where is the classic S/A/B/C table?**
Deliberately omitted. With hotfixes landing weekly and rankings moving, a frozen letter table would be false precision. The grouped consensus above ages better.

**Which class is best for solo play?**
Viking is the community's default answer — forgiving, durable, and self-sufficient through the campaign and early endgame.

**Which class is best for co-op?**
Ranged classes with strong area damage (Marksman, Pyromancer) slot naturally into co-op, where a melee teammate can hold attention.

**My class got nerfed mid-season. Now what?**
Check the official 7.0.x notes for the specific change, then look at what the community adapted — build-defining nerfs usually produce a new standard build within days.
