## Damage That Travels Between Enemies

The Stormweaver's defining mechanic is propagation: its lightning attacks don't just hit a target, they **arc onward** to nearby enemies. This gives the class a distinctive power curve — against a lone enemy it is merely good, and against a screen full of demons it is spectacular, because every additional body is another link in the chain. Learning to see maps as *chain networks* rather than target lists is the class's whole skill expression.

Community coverage for Season 10 rates the Stormweaver in the strong-starter band (LagoFast's rankings name it directly alongside the [Marksman](/guides/hero-siege-marksman-build/), [Paladin](/guides/hero-siege-paladin-build/) and [Nomad](/guides/hero-siege-nomad-build/)), and it shares caster duties with the [Pyromancer](/guides/hero-siege-pyromancer-build/) as the season's lightning-flavored alternative.

## Reading Maps as Chain Networks

A Pyromancer asks "where is the pack?" A Stormweaver asks "how do the packs **connect**?" That reframing changes everything about how you route:

- **Tight corridors and doorways are your amplifier** — enemies bunched by geometry make chains reach their full length.
- **Sparse arena fights are your weak spot** — bosses and spread-out elites starve the propagation. Carry one non-chaining single-target skill for these, or plan to grind them honestly.
- **Your positioning feeds the chains** — standing where enemies stream toward you in a line turns their own pathing into your damage geometry.

This is why the class rewards patience over reflexes: you are not dodging and bursting, you are *arranging traffic* and letting physics do the work.

## Rotation and Talents

The Stormweaver's rotation is a rhythm: a spammable arcing attack that keeps chains alive, a heavier lightning strike on cooldown for dense moments, and a control or escape tool for the exceptions. The loop is steady rather than explosive — think metronome, not drum solo.

Talent priorities follow from the mechanic:

1. **Chain-count and bounce nodes first.** More links per cast is the class's core multiplier; nothing else scales as directly.
2. **Lightning damage broads second.** These feed every link in every chain, compounding with gear across the whole season.
3. **Resource sustain third.** A metronome that stops for mana is a metronome that dies; upkeep nodes keep the rhythm unbroken through long maps.
4. **The defensive underlayer always.** Caster health pools are thin; a mitigation band from the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) and a habit of range discipline cover the class's one weakness.

## Gear: Feeding the Arcs

- **Lightning-damage and cast-speed affixes** are the engine — faster casting keeps chains perpetually active across the map.
- **Area and chain-extending uniques** are the build's chase items; the [market guide](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) explains how to buy them without overpaying mid-season.
- **Defensive pieces** stay thin but present — enough to survive one mistake per arena, not enough to change the playstyle. The [uniques overview](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) orients you in the rarity ladder before shopping.

## Endgame Scaling

The Stormweaver's endgame rides the same rails as every caster, with one class-specific twist:

1. **Density is your multiplier** — [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) branches that increase spawns are effectively damage nodes for this class, more than for any burst archetype.
2. **[Wormhole pushing](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/)** at depth, where density peaks, is where the build feels strongest; single-target guardian phases are the counterweight, handled by your burst slot or a patient chain-into-the-boss approach.
3. **[Uber attempts](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/)** — arena size matters; learn each arena's pull spots to rebuild chains during the fight.
4. **[Farming](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/)** — the sustained clear style maps neatly onto gold-per-hour loops once the tree is filled.

## FAQ

**Stormweaver or Pyromancer — which caster should I pick?**
Pick by damage texture: fire for burst and screen-wipes, lightning for sustained chains through density. Both are top-band casters this season; the [Pyromancer](/guides/hero-siege-pyromancer-build/) page covers the fire path in depth.

**Does the class work against single bosses?**
It works, without shining. Chains need neighbors; boss fights are handled with your burst slot and patience, or by adding adds-friendly arenas to your farm routes.

**Is Stormweaver new-player friendly?**
Moderately — the kit is simple, but the "arrange traffic" mindset takes a few sessions. New players wanting the absolute simplest caster start should compare notes with the [beginner guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) first.

**What's the build's biggest power spike?**
Chain-extending nodes and uniques. Every extra link multiplies the value of every other link — the class's scaling is unusually steep through the mid-tree.
