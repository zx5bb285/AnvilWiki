## What Is the Incarnation Tree?

The Incarnation Tree is the largest progression system in Hero Siege: a sprawling web of **over 1,600 passive nodes**, introduced at the start of Season 9 on April 3, 2026, and still central to endgame power in Season 10. Where your class talent tree defines how your character plays, the Incarnation Tree defines how far your character scales — it is the long-term account of your season's grind.

It unlocked to a mixed reception: the community's first reaction ranged from excitement at the sheer scale to concern about grind length, and Panic Art Studio published a dedicated video response addressing that feedback during Season 9. Two seasons in, it has settled into its role: the system you work on steadily for an entire league.

## How the Tree Works

The structure follows the genre's established conventions:

- **You earn points by playing.** Endgame activity — wormholes, boss kills, seasonal objectives — feeds the point economy that lets you claim nodes.
- **Nodes form paths.** Like most modern passive webs, you travel between nodes along connected paths, so allocation order matters: rushing a deep, powerful node means passing up many small ones along the way.
- **Choices compound.** Small-looking stat nodes add up across hundreds of allocations; the tree's real power is in the aggregate, not in any single pickup.

The exact point curve — how many points per hour of play, how fast early allocations come — has been adjusted in patches since Season 9, so treat any specific number you read (including in older guides) as provisional.

## A Sensible First-Week Allocation Strategy

Exact builds depend on your class and goals, but the shape of a good first pass is consistent across the community's advice:

1. **Take the defensive spine first.** Early deaths waste more points-per-minute than any offense node earns you. Life, mitigation, and sustain nodes keep you alive while the tree fills in.
2. **Prioritize quality-of-life and find-radius style nodes early.** Anything that makes each run smoother pays itself back across hundreds of runs.
3. **Delay the expensive keystone-style nodes.** Deep, build-defining nodes are worth taking once your [build's core items](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) exist in your inventory — not before, when your build can still pivot.
4. **Coordinate with your seasonal systems.** The [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) shapes what your runs look like; the Incarnation Tree should amplify the run style you have settled on, not fight it.

## Incarnation Tree and Season 10

Season 10's own systems layer on top of the Incarnation Tree rather than replacing it: the talent tree expansion changed what your class does with its baseline power, while Act 9, the [new Uber bosses](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/), and the reworked [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) changed what you are spending that power on. If you are returning from Season 9, your tree knowledge transfers — only the meta around it moved.

## Common Mistakes

- **Respecting spreadsheets over playstyle.** A node path that makes runs feel awful is never worth it, whatever its theoretical value.
- **Ignoring the tree until "later."** Points not being earned while you dither are points lost; claim them as you play.
- **Reading Season 9 guides for numbers.** Balance passes since April have touched the tree. Structure advice ages; specific node values do not.

## FAQ

**Is the Incarnation Tree account-wide or per character?**
It is a per-character progression system within the season, earned through that character's play. Verify the current rollover rules in the official season notes, as seasonal persistence has varied by system.

**Can I respec Incarnation points?**
Respec options have changed across patches — check your character panel in-game for the current cost and mechanism rather than relying on older guides.

**Is the grind worth it?**
If you plan to play the season for more than a couple of weeks, yes: the tree is where long-term power lives, and every endgame goal — [wormhole pushes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/), Uber kills — assumes a filling tree behind your build.

**Where can I see the full tree?**
In-game at level 100. Third-party tree viewers exist but lag patches; the in-game view is the source of truth.
