## What Is a Chaos Tower?

Chaos Towers are Hero Siege's dedicated endgame farm structure — repeatable tower content whose reward curve is why two separate creators made dedicated farming guides about it in one season. The loop is simple to describe and demanding to execute: enter, clear the tower's challenges as fast as your build allows, collect the payout, repeat. Community documentation for [Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) puts optimized tower routes at **over a million gold per hour** — the number in the most-watched farm video's title is a real route, not clickbait.

<Callout type="info" title="Rates are snapshots, not promises">
Gold-per-hour figures come from creator documentation on specific builds at specific patches. 7.0.x balance changes move tower rewards and clear speeds alike — treat any rate you read as the farm's ceiling, not your entitlement. Verify in-game before budgeting around it.
</Callout>

## How a Tower Farm Route Works

The community's tower guides share the same loop anatomy:

1. **Enter on a fast-clearing build.** Tower farming is a clear-speed economy — the [class tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) is effectively a farm-rate table for this content.
2. **Clear in a fixed route.** Optimized runs follow a repeatable path through the tower's challenges rather than improvising — the difference between a documented million-gold hour and a casual one is route discipline.
3. **Bank the payout on a schedule.** Farming in focused blocks with a [market purchase](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) in sight converts gold into gear power instead of a stagnating pile.
4. **Re-time the route after every patch.** Balance shifts change what "optimized" means — the [patch tracker](/guides/hero-siege-patch-notes/) is part of the farm kit.

## Where Tower Farming Sits in the Gold Economy

Season 10's endgame offers three serious [gold](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) engines, and they answer different problems:

| Method | Variance | Best for | Guides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chaos Towers** | Medium | Builds with fast tower clears | This page |
| **Mining** | Low | Any build, steady income, no gear gate | [Mining guide](/guides/hero-siege-mining-guide/) |
| **Market trading** | Skill-based | Players with economy knowledge | [Market guide](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) |

The community's standard routine stacks them: mining covers the floor while the build is still growing, tower farming takes over once clears are fast, and market knowledge multiplies whatever both produce. What the routine should not do is pick one method for a whole season — the [economy](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) shifts around patches, and the farm rotation shifts with it.

## When to Start Tower Farming

The entry signal is the same as every endgame farm: **your build clears the content without dying and without stalls.** Tower content is tuned for [endgame-tier](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) characters — a transitioning build farms towers slower than it farms [mining routes](/guides/hero-siege-mining-guide/), which is the tell that it is not ready. Get the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) behind you first; the towers pay for patience with the best hourly rate in the game.

## Chaos Tower FAQ

### Do towers need a party or do they work solo?

Community farm guides document solo routes as the standard — solo keeps the whole loop and its income under one player's control. Group play changes the math, but the published million-gold benchmarks are solo numbers.

### Which classes farm towers fastest?

The honest answer changes with every balance patch, which is why the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) exists. Fast-clearing builds of any archetype out-farm slow ones — the farm rewards the stat, not the class name.

### Is tower farming still viable after the latest patch?

Viability has held across the 7.0.x line so far, but the rates move — check the [patch tracker](/guides/hero-siege-patch-notes/) and one fresh farming video before recommitting a farming week to towers.
