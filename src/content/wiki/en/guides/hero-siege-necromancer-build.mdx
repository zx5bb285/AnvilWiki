## The Only Race That Actually Matters

Hero Siege resets every season, and the first days of a season are a race — not against other players, but against the boring part. The faster a class clears the campaign and the early [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/), the sooner it starts earning, and the sooner its [gear](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) stops being whatever dropped. In that race, community tier discussion hands the [Necromancer](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) one specific build: **Bone Shred**, the S-tier leveling answer.

This page is about that build — why it levels the way it does, and what its speed is and is not worth.

## Why Bone Shred Levels So Fast

The community's description of Bone Shred points at the thing that makes any ARPG leveling build good: it **puts the damage where the packs are** without demanding setup or a perfect rotation. Leveling is not boss damage — it is miles of trash, and a build that clears trash efficiently will always out-level a build that plans for a fight it will not reach for hours.

A leveling build earns its tier by three properties, and Bone Shred is described as having all three:

- **Low setup.** The kit works while your gear is random drops and your tree is half-empty.
- **Early power.** It comes online during the campaign rather than waiting for a key item.
- **Room to spare.** It leaves nodes for the escape hatches leveling players actually need — movement and mitigation.

Bone Shred's reputation comes from community tier discussion of Season 10, not from a published skill spreadsheet. The build is real and the ranking is community-held; the node-by-node detail is still being documented. Level it and let the class teach you where the damage actually lives.

## The Route Bone Shred Implies

Fast leveling is not a talent page — it is a sequence, and Bone Shred's reputation implies one:

1. **Push campaign levels without detouring.** The whole value of the build is time saved; stopping to farm early throws that away. The [beginner guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) covers the campaign's real checkpoints.
2. **Climb the ladder while it is cheap.** Normal and Nightmare are where a leveling build feels strongest — the Necromancer spends them ahead of the curve, banking [gold](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) your slower classes cannot yet.
3. **Bank the early economy.** Season starts are won with a [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) advantage bought before prices settle. The head start is the real reward; spend it deliberately.
4. **Then decide what the character becomes.** The [league starter page](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) maps where fast levelers go — some re-spec into an endgame identity, some get parked.

## Where "Leveling King" Stops Being the Point

Here is the honest turn most "S-tier leveling" writeups avoid: **fastest to the meta is not the same as strongest in it.** A leveling build is optimized for a stretch of the game you leave behind, and the questions that decide a season's real ceiling — single-target pressure, [Uber](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) readiness, how the build survives when standing still is fatal — are answered elsewhere.

That does not make Bone Shred a trap. It makes it a tool. If your plan is to race a fresh economy and convert the head start into gear, a leveling-king class is exactly right. If your plan is to pick one class and take it to the deepest content this season, the leveling tier is a starting credential, not a verdict — and the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) should be read with that distinction in hand.

## Necromancer FAQ

### Should I level a Necromancer just to fund another character?

It is one of the better reasons to play it. A fast leveler produces gold and a mapped ladder early, and a season's [economy](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) rewards whoever reaches the market first. If your true main is slow to start, a Bone Shred Necromancer can be the thing that pays for it.

### Does Bone Shred need specific gear to function?

Its whole appeal as a leveling build is that it functions on what drops. Gear is an upgrade path, not a gate — if a specific item were required, the build would not be the S-tier *leveling* pick in the first place.

### How does leveling speed compare across the season's free classes?

Community discussion ranks leveling builds across the whole roster, not just the free ones, and Bone Shred sits at the top of it. The [league starter page](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) is the better place to compare the free options head to head, since price and leveling speed are different axes.
