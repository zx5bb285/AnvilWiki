## The Page That Starts With an Admission

Every other class page on this site can open by describing what the class does. This one cannot, and pretending otherwise would be the worst thing a wiki could do. **The Exo is the least-documented class on Hero Siege's roster.** Community notes on it are sparse — noticeably thinner than the pages for classes that came free in Season 9 or Season 10, and thinner even than the other quiet corners of the roster.

That is the actual subject of this page. A guide for the Exo would be dishonest if it were written as though a normal amount of information existed, so instead this page documents the gap itself: what is recorded, why the rest is missing, and what that means if the class is on your shortlist anyway.

<Callout type="warn" title="Read this before you expect a build">
There is no skill-by-skill breakdown below, no stat priority, and no gear list — not because the author is being coy, but because the Season 10 source material for the Exo does not exist at the depth required to write them honestly. Anything more specific on this page would be invented.
</Callout>

## Scorching Whip: The One Thing on Record

One ability is consistently attached to the Exo in community material: **Scorching Whip**. It is the class's documented anchor, and for most of the roster an anchor like that would be a starting point for a much longer page.

Here it is the whole of the verified picture. This guide will state the name, because the name is sourced, and will stop there, because anything about how it behaves in practice — its range, its scaling, how it fits a rotation — is not something Season 10 documentation currently supports. Check it in-game on the skill tooltip; for now that tooltip is a better source than any guide.

If you are wondering whether that is a suspiciously small amount of information for a class on a 24-class roster: yes, it is. The next section is about why.

## Why Some Classes Get Documented and Others Do Not

Guide coverage is not distributed by design merit. It follows population, and population follows a handful of boring factors:

- **Price changes pull attention.** The classes that went free in Season 9 — [Samurai](/guides/hero-siege-samurai-build/), Shaman, Demon Slayer and others — got a wave of new players and, with them, a wave of questions worth answering. Classes outside those changes did not.
- **Content creators need an audience.** A class gets a video or a write-up when someone believes people will watch or read it. The Exo has not attracted that bet.
- **Benchmarks create their own coverage.** A class like the [Demon Slayer](/guides/hero-siege-demon-slayer-build/), used as the community's boss-damage yardstick, gets discussed constantly as a side effect of being the comparison point. A class nobody compares against generates no such byproduct.
- **Early impressions stick.** A class that made a weak first impression in an old season keeps that reputation long after tuning has moved on, and old reputations suppress new write-ups.

The Exo's thin record is a consequence of those forces, not evidence about the class's strength. That distinction is worth holding onto — sparse documentation is often mistaken for a verdict.

## Playing a Class Nobody Has Written Up

If you are going to play the Exo, go in with the right expectations rather than a borrowed build you cannot verify. Two practical consequences:

1. **You will be your own source.** The [beginner guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) and the [Ether Tree guide](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) teach the general systems every class uses. Use them for the framework, and treat the Exo's own tooltips as the authority on the Exo.
2. **You will not know if a build is correct, only if it works.** Without a community baseline, success is measured by clearing content, not by matching someone else's list. That is a real disadvantage in efficiency and, for some players, a genuinely interesting way to play.

The class also stays outside the free-class changes reported for Season 9 and Season 10, so it is not a class that got pulled into the spotlight by a pricing decision. Whether that changes in a later season is exactly the kind of thing that would finally produce the documentation this page cannot.

## A Deliberately Cautious Progression Outline

No invented steps — just the frame that holds for any class, with an Exo-specific warning at each stage:

- **Campaign to level 100.** This is the most valuable Exo resource available: uninterrupted, self-paced time with the class and its tooltips. Read what the game tells you, because a [guide](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) will not tell you.
- **Test on the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/).** Push until something stops working, and note what stopped. That note is the closest thing to a build guide the class currently has.
- **Follow the [league starter](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) discipline for the systems.** Committing to one damage plan through the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) is correct for every class, including the undocumented ones.
- **Treat the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) as a floor, not a ceiling.** A rarely played class is rarely sampled, and rarely sampled classes are ranked with less confidence than they deserve.

## Exo FAQ

### Is the Exo a bad class?

Unknown is not the same as bad. The class sits in a part of the roster the community has not measured properly, and this page will not convert missing information into a negative judgement — that is the same error as inventing a build.

### What should I do if I want an Exo guide that does not exist?

Level the class and write down what you find; that is how every other class's documentation started. In the meantime, the [official patch notes](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) and in-game tooltips are the only two Exo sources worth trusting.

### Will this page be expanded?

It will be updated when there is something to add. If community notes on the Exo appear at usable depth, this page grows into a normal class guide. Until then, the honest version is the one you are reading.
