## How Do Ether Quests Work?

Ether Quests are the bridge between playing the game and powering the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) — the progression layer [Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) expanded by about a hundred nodes. The chain presents objectives across the endgame, and completing them feeds the Ether progression that keeps paying out for the rest of the season. Unlike a one-time reward, the value compounds: every node the chain unlocks makes your build permanently better at the content the next objective asks for.

<Callout type="info" title="Community-documented chain">
Hero Siege does not publish official quest documentation, so the flow below reflects how dedicated guides (and the community's completion videos) currently describe the chain. Objective details and node values shift with 7.0.x patches — verify in-game before planning around a specific step.
</Callout>

## When Should You Start the Chain?

The practical start signal, consistent across guides: **campaign finished, level 100, first endgame build assembled.** The objectives are tuned for characters already working through the upper [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) — starting earlier mostly produces frustration, and the rewards mean little to a build that is still transitioning.

That placement is deliberate design, and it mirrors how the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) was introduced in Season 9: long-system progression unlocks when the player has a build worth investing in.

## The Completion Order That Works

Documented runs of the chain converge on the same shape:

1. **Pick up the chain early in your endgame routine** — the first objectives double as a tour of the systems you should already be touching: endgame zones, [farming routes](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/), boss content.
2. **Batch objectives with your farming loop.** The chain is designed to be completed alongside normal endgame play, not instead of it — players who detour to quest objectives exclusively lose the farming income that funds the rest of the season.
3. **Feed everything into the tree, not the market.** Ether progression is account-season power; early [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) temptations are cheaper to satisfy with [gold farming](/guides/hero-siege-mining-guide/) than with stalled quest chains.
4. **Re-check after every patch.** The 7.0.x line has already touched Ether-adjacent systems — the [patch tracker](/guides/hero-siege-patch-notes/) lists what moved.

## What Ether Progression Actually Buys

The reason the chain matters is the tree it feeds. Season 10's Ether expansion turned the system into one of the largest long-term power sources in the game — comparable in scale to the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) rollout that defined Season 9 — and the community's endgame builds assume the nodes are flowing. A completed or progressing Ether Tree shows up in three places:

- **Clear speed** — offensive nodes compound with your [build's](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) core scaling.
- **Farming efficiency** — faster clears convert directly into [gold](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) and drop volume.
- **Endgame access** — the top of the [Uber roster](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) and deep [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pushes assume endgame-level character power, of which the Ether Tree is a major slice.

## Ether Quests FAQ

### Can I complete Ether Quests on any difficulty?

The objectives track endgame content, which lives in the upper ladder. Community guidance is to progress the chain alongside your natural [difficulty climb](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) rather than farming it at a tier your build outgrew.

### Do I need a specific class for the chain?

No. The chain is class-agnostic, though clear speed varies across the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) as always — faster builds finish objective-heavy stretches sooner.

### What if an objective seems impossible?

Check three things, in order: whether a 7.0.x patch changed the requirement (the [patch tracker](/guides/hero-siege-patch-notes/)), whether your build is under-geared for the objective's tier, and whether the community has documented a route trick. If all three fail, the chain will still be there after your build catches up — it is a season-long system, not a race.
