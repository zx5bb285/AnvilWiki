## The Bruiser's Bargain

Every Hero Siege class makes you a offer: trade something — range, tempo, safety — for a damage plan. The Butcher's offer is the most traditional in the game: give up range, get relentless. The class works at contact distance, sustains its output while packs stay in range, and asks in return that you enjoy the inside of a fight.

That trade is why the class's community documentation centers on the [campaign](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/): a melee kit with continuous clear is exactly what leveling rewards, and the class's [S10 leveling guides](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) document the route end to end.

## The Campaign Leveling Flow

The class's documented leveling shape has four beats:

1. **Learn the sustain loop early.** The Butcher's kit wants continuous engagement — the faster you learn which packs you can chain without pausing, the faster the campaign moves.
2. **Spend skill points into one coherent kit.** The classic melee mistake is spreading points across every tool; the [league starter](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) discipline applies from level one.
3. **Let the acts teach positioning.** Ranged classes learn positioning to stay away; the Butcher learns it to stay productive — the same [act-boss lessons](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/), different geometry.
4. **Exit the campaign with a plan, not just a level.** Level 100 with an identity beats level 100 with trivia — the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) and [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) compound whichever plan you fed them.

## The Transition Points

A Butcher becomes an endgame build at the same three checkpoints every class faces:

- **The [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/).** The bruiser's natural durability carries the mid-ladder; Nightmare and Hell start asking whether the damage plan is real.
- **The endgame economy.** [Wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pushes and [Uber](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) rotations are gear-checks and gear-sources at once — the [farming loop](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) that upgrades the build.
- **The slot map.** The class's [BiS logic](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) follows its melee identity: damage slots first, the durability band second, everything else in farming order — with the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) closing gaps the drops miss.

## Butcher FAQ

### Butcher or Viking for melee fans?

The classic comparison. The [Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) is the community's documented beginner default with deeper guide coverage; the Butcher is the more aggressive expression of the same melee instinct. Try both — the one whose rhythm fits your hands wins.

### Is the Butcher viable in Season 10's endgame?

Viable, with the usual honest caveats: every class's S10 standing lives on the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/), and endgame viability is earned by build completion rather than class label. The class clears what completed builds clear.

### Where does the class struggle?

Where melee always struggles: burst phases that punish contact distance, and content designed around kiting. The fixes are the standard ones — [durability slots](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/), movement practice, and refusing to fight on the boss's terms.
