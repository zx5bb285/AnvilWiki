## The Class That Adapts to Your Loot

Most Hero Siege classes decide their build for you: the talent tree points one direction and your gear follows. The Paladin is the exception — its kit is built around flexibility, supporting both a **heavy melee weapon path** and a **holy-damage casting path** inside the same class shell. In practice this changes your first-week decisions: instead of locking a build at character creation, you play the campaign, watch what your drops and the market offer, and let the loot choose the path.

Community tier coverage for Season 10 rates the Paladin a strong A-tier starter on that basis — not the flashiest ceiling, but the fewest bad loot outcomes. Our [Season 10 class tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) tracks where the class sits as balance patches move it.

<Callout type="info" title="Two paths, one foundation">
Whichever direction you take, the Paladin's base durability and utility skills serve both paths. Early talents that improve survivability and resource efficiency are never wasted, whichever way the gear breaks.
</Callout>

## The Two Paths Compared

**The melee path — hammer-first.** The classic heritage build (the community's "Hammerdin" lineage from earlier seasons): heavy two-handed weapons, melee area strikes, and talent nodes that reward standing in the fray. Choose it when your finds skew toward strong melee bases and attack-speed or area affixes.

- Plays closest to the [Viking's](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) comfort zone but with more utility woven in — holy-flavored skills add damage or control on top of the weapon swings.
- Scales through raw weapon upgrades, so it consumes [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) purchases efficiently when a good base appears.

**The caster path — holy at range.** The lighter, more technical direction: holy-damage casts layered at mid-range while the class's natural durability covers positioning mistakes. Choose it when caster-style items and cast-affix gear show up first.

- Plays like a sturdier cousin of the [Pyromancer](/guides/hero-siege-pyromancer-build/): less burst ceiling, far more forgiveness.
- Benefits disproportionately from the defensive [Incarnation nodes](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) because it fights closer than a pure caster would.

## Reading Your Loot: the First-Path Decision

A simple triage for the first ten levels of talent points:

1. **Opened a strong melee base with area or attack-speed affixes?** Lean melee; take the weapon-damage band first.
2. **Found cast-affix gear or a holy-scaling unique?** Lean caster; take the holy-damage band first.
3. **Found nothing notable?** Build the shared foundation — survivability and utility — and stay flexible. The Paladin is one of the few classes where delaying commitment is a real strategy rather indecision.

## Season 10 Context

Season 10's talent expansion touched every class, and the Paladin gained the most from tree depth in the mid-bands — more room to blend paths without starving either. Community tier coverage (MetaRoad, LagoFast and the dedicated creators) keeps it in the starter-recommendation conversation alongside the [Marksman](/guides/hero-siege-marksman-build/) and [Nomad](/guides/hero-siege-nomad-build/), which makes it a low-regret pick for players who refuse to pick.

Endgame transition is conventional: level 100 opens the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/), the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) shapes your farming runs, and the class's balance carries it into [wormholes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) and the [Uber ladder](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) without a rebuild.

## Common Mistakes

- **Half-committing both paths early.** Flexibility is a mid-game luxury; early talent points split across both directions leave the campaign slower than either focused path.
- **Ignoring utility skills as "not damage."** The Paladin's controls and self-sufficiency are why it survives the difficulties where glass builds stall.
- **Forcing a path against your loot.** The class's whole design rewards adaptation — fighting your drops is swimming upstream.

## FAQ

**Is the Paladin tanky enough to facetank like a Viking?**
Tougher than any caster, softer than the Viking. It fights at melee-caster hybrid range — close enough to apply pressure, disciplined enough to step out.

**Does the Paladin have a support or party-healing role in co-op?**
Its utility skills help groups, but it is not a dedicated healer — think "durable damage-dealer with tools," consistent with its solo design.

**Which path has the higher endgame ceiling?**
Community consensus favors the melee path at the very top end, but both clear all content; the caster path's consistency often wins on time-to-clear across a whole season.

**When should I respec between paths?**
After a gear-changing session — a new unique or a set of market purchases is the natural respec trigger, not a feeling.
