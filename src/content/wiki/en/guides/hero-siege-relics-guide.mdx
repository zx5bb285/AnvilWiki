## What Relics Do

Relics are Hero Siege's compounding investment. A unique item is powerful the moment it drops; a relic starts modest and grows — upgrade by upgrade, to level 10 — into one of the most reliable power sources in your [endgame](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) kit. That time dimension is the whole design: relics reward players who farm consistently, which is why every "get your relics" guide for [Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) opens with the same advice — start early, upgrade steadily.

<Callout type="info" title="Community-documented system">
Relic upgrade costs and drop sources are not covered by official documentation. The practices below summarize what dedicated relic guides currently recommend; verify costs in-game as 7.0.x patches continue to land.
</Callout>

## How to Get Relics

The community's farming advice clusters around three habits:

1. **Play the endgame loop.** Relic sources tie into the content you are already doing at level 100 — [Uber encounters](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/), high-difficulty runs and endgame farming routes.
2. **Farm in sessions, not in minutes.** Multiple maxing guides converge on the same point: relics respond to volume. Short sporadic runs produce little; dedicated farming blocks produce visible upgrade progress.
3. **Don't spread early income across every relic.** Concentrate on the relics your build actually scales with first, then widen.

## Upgrading to Level 10

The upgrade track to level 10 is where most of a relic's value lives. The community's batch-upgrade advice exists because early levels are cheap and the last levels are not — dumping everything into one relic's final levels leaves the rest of your collection stalled. A steadier pattern: raise your core relics evenly through the cheap levels, then push final levels on the ones your build leans on hardest.

What each upgrade grants varies by relic, and exact per-level values are the least-documented part of the system. Treat specific numbers from older guides as pre-Ebontharn data until you see them in-game.

## Relic Storage: Season 10's Quality-of-Life Change

Before Season 10, a growing relic collection competed with everything else for inventory and stash space. The [Ebontharn update](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) added **Relic Storage**, a dedicated home for the collection — which matters more than it sounds, because relic farming only compounds if you never have a reason to stop collecting.

If you are returning to the game after a break, Relic Storage is the system change most likely to affect how you organize your endgame loot routine; the [returning player guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) walks through the rest of the S10 landscape.

## Relic Mistakes to Avoid

- **Leveling trophies, not build pieces.** A relic your build does not scale with is decoration at level 10.
- **Ignoring the farming side.** Guides that promise quick maxing all assume one thing first: volume of endgame runs. There is no crafting shortcut around it.
- **Selling or discarding duplicates before checking storage space.** With Relic Storage now in the game, collection management is cheaper than ever — use it before making permanent decisions.

## Relic FAQ

### Are relics character-bound or account-wide?

Scope details are among the least-documented parts of the system, and community answers have shifted across seasons — verify in-game on your own collection before planning around either assumption.

### Do relics matter before level 100?

Marginally. Your early levels are better spent learning your build and pushing the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/). Relic farming becomes a real priority in the endgame, when steady power growth starts competing with gear luck.

### Where do relic guides disagree?

On farming efficiency — which route or boss yields best. They agree on the fundamentals: farm endgame content in volume, upgrade in batches, and prioritize build-relevant relics. Where numbers conflict, in-game testing wins.
