## The Season's Quietest Big Change

Season 10's headline systems — the [Ether expansion](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/), the [Nightmare rework](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/), the [new Uber layer](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) — took the spotlight, but the change with the widest reach may be the free-class expansion: **Marauder**, [Plague Doctor](/guides/hero-siege-plague-doctor-build/), [Shield Lancer](/guides/hero-siege-shield-lancer-build/) and the **Jötunn** all joined the free roster with Ebontharn, per community season coverage.

For the class itself, the shorthand is heavyweight: big, deliberate melee whose identity reads through its name — and whose appeal is exactly the thing heavyweight classes always offer, the feeling of every hit mattering.

## Who the Jötunn Fits

Heavyweight melee is a taste, and the class knows it. The fit is players who:

- **Prefer committed swings to rapid spam** — deliberate attack rhythms over button-mashing tempos.
- **Want position choices to matter.** Heavyweight classes trade tempo for impact, which makes each engagement a positioning decision.
- **Are returning players eyeing a new main.** A free heavyweight is the lowest-cost way to change how the game feels — the [season restart guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) covers the rest of the returning-player checklist.

It is the least reflexive pick in the free roster — the [Marauder](/guides/hero-siege-marauder-build/) is its mirror opposite — which is precisely why the free expansion made the roster stronger as a set: four temperaments, zero cost.

## Starting a Jötunn Season

1. **Campaign as the tutorial it is.** Learn the swing timing on content that forgives it — the [beginner guide's](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) leveling route applies, and heavyweight classes level fine on the standard path.
2. **Climb the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) with the class's natural durability.** Heavyweight melee's margin shows up in the mid-ladder, where [Nightmare's rework](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) starts checking builds.
3. **Feed the long systems early.** The [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) and [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) are season-long investments; a heavyweight identity benefits from the defensive spines as much as any class.
4. **Farm into the endgame economy** — [wormholes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/), the [Uber rotation](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/), and the [gold routine](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) that turns the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) into a gear slot.

## Jötunn FAQ

### Is the Jötunn strong in the Season 10 meta?

The free roster's classes are all viable, and where the Jötunn's kill times land in the current meta is still settling — the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) tracks that as community data accumulates. What is settled: the class is free, playable end-to-end, and no longer a purchase decision.

### Does a slow attack style work in fast endgame content?

The concern is standard for heavyweight classes, and the standard answer holds: endgame is a clear-speed economy, and heavy hitters pay for tempo with impact per swing. Whether the trade feels good is a session-one question — free means the trial costs nothing.
