## Why Gold Matters More in Season 10

Gold is Hero Siege's trading currency, and Season 10 made it more central than any recent season: the **reworked market** turned player trading into a first-class gear source, and the **Ether Tree's economy branches** — including chance nodes buffed in the 7.0.x patches — made every run's gold income a knob you control. A player who understands the economy reaches their build's endgame form weeks before one who only farms drops.

Sleepy Cat's week-by-week economy videos have become the community's seasonal reference for how gold income scales — early weeks reward different methods than mid-season. This page follows the same structure: the right farm for the right phase.

<Callout type="warn" title="Economy numbers decay fast">
Specific gold-per-hour figures circulating in videos and posts reflect the week they were recorded and the patch it ran on. Treat any number — including community claims of "millions per hour" — as a directional claim until you time it yourself.
</Callout>

## Method 1: Ether-Boosted Clear Loops (Week 1 Onward)

The season's foundation farm. Fill the [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) economy branches early — the Treasure Chest and Presence of Evil nodes were explicitly buffed in September's patch notes — then run your fastest comfortable clearing loop.

- **Why it works:** the nodes raise the value of content you were clearing anyway; zero new skills required.
- **Best for:** every player, especially on a new [league starter](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/).
- **Pairs with:** quest completion, since [Ether Quests](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) pay out regardless of run quality.

## Method 2: Mining (Low Investment, Steady)

Mining is the game's quiet gold engine. Community guides have documented multi-million yields from dedicated mining runs, and it requires no build power beyond movement speed.

- **Why it works:** mineral nodes respawn on reliable cycles, and the activity is nearly risk-free.
- **Best for:** players with slow or mid-progress builds; alt-tab-friendly sessions; anyone priced out of the market early.
- **Watch for:** route videos age fast — find a current-season mining route rather than an old one, since node placement follows the map changes of recent acts like Act 9.

## Method 3: Chaos Tower Runs (Mid-Season Power Spike)

Chaos Towers are a mid-season farm that several creators (ItsAdiar, Sleepy Cat) have documented as a strong income source once your build can clear them quickly.

- **Why it works:** towers bundle dense combat with high-value objectives; fast builds convert them into serious per-hour income.
- **Best for:** the phase after your first big gear milestones, when clearing speed stops being the bottleneck.
- **Note:** Season 10's patch notes added a Whispering Prophet Ether node to Chaos Pillars — check whether your tree route passes through it before planning a tower-focused session.

## Method 4: Wormhole Farming (Late-Season Compounding)

At depth, [wormholes](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) become a gold farm: dense drops plus guardian loot, sold via the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/). This is the compounding phase — every gold-funded gear upgrade raises your clear speed, which raises your income.

- **Why it works:** it converts your pushing progress into liquidity instead of just personal drops.
- **Best for:** builds near their endgame form with the [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) filling in.
- **Watch for:** depth sweet spots — farm where you clear fast, not at your record depth.

## What NOT to Do

- **Vendor grinding for gold.** Selling trash drops one loop at a time is the slowest income in the game; it only makes sense as a byproduct of real farming.
- **Ignoring the market entirely.** In Season 10, self-found purity has a real price. Gap-filling one or two slots via the [market guide](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) is usually cheaper than grinding them.
- **Buying before pricing.** Market prices move daily in a live season. Check current listings before assuming a video's "cheap upgrade" advice still holds.

## FAQ

**How much gold should I keep in reserve?**
Enough to buy a key item at a moment's notice — the community rule of thumb is to hold your last big purchase's worth of gold as liquid, and invest income above that.

**Does gold carry between characters?**
Gold is shared at the account/stash level like other currencies; verify the exact sharing rules in-game as seasonal structures have changed before.

**What sells best on the market?**
Mid-tier gear with good rolls moves fastest — endgame buyers gap-filling builds. Top-rarity uniques sell for headline prices but slowly.

**Is there any gold sink I should plan for?**
Respecs and crafting. Budget for experimentation — the ability to retool a build mid-season is worth more than any single purchase.
