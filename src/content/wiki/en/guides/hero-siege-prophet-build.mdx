## A Class With No Consensus Build Yet

The Prophet is the newest thing in Hero Siege — a Season 10 addition and the only class on the [roster](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) whose written history points the wrong way. She is voiced by Dela, and she plays as three ideas stacked: a **summoner**, a **mage** who commands roots and boughs, and a **shapeshifter** with three distinct forms. That combination is genuinely new; it is also why this page reads differently from every other class guide on the site.

<Callout type="info" title="This page will not invent a skill list">
Season 10 is weeks old and community documentation of the Prophet is still forming. Everything below is drawn from what community sources have written so far — summons, forms, and the Season 10 clear-tool nerf. Where a number or a node name would normally appear, we say pending instead. Verify specifics in-game before committing a season.

## What the Prophet Actually Is

Strip the marketing back and the class does three jobs:

- **She summons.** Community documentation names the **Carrion Worm**, **Ravens**, **Ents**, and a **Spirit of the Forest** among her companions. This is a class that fights with a body count, not a single rotation.
- **She commands the ground.** Her core toolkit is described as branches and roots — which is why her play is read as a mage who controls space rather than a caster who only deals damage from the back line.
- **She changes shape.** Three documented forms: **Wendigo** (melee claw attacks), **Ent or Bear** (a durability/frontline mode), and **Storm Hawk** (chain lightning, plus the ability to fly over terrain).

That third pillar is the one that separates the Prophet from the [Stormweaver](/guides/hero-siege-stormweaver-build/) and other lightning-flavored classes: Storm Hawk's flight is a movement mechanic, not just a damage mode, and it rewrites how you take a map.

## The Season 9 Builds Are a Trap

The most important sentence on this page: **the community reports that Season 10 reduced the strength of the Prophet's clearing tools.** Most Prophet material you can find online predates that change. Copying a Season 9 Prophet build wholesale means importing a plan for a clear speed she no longer has.

The correct response is not to abandon the class. It is to treat her as new — to level her, watch which of her tools still pull their weight in 7.0.x, and build around the answer. That is a slower start than following a finished guide, and it is the honest one.

## The Three Forms, and What Each Is For

| Form | Combat role | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Wendigo** | Melee pressure | Claw attacks; puts you in contact range |
| **Ent / Bear** | Defense and frontline | Bulk and survivability |
| **Storm Hawk** | Ranged burst + mobility | Chain lightning; flight over terrain |

The design lesson is that the Prophet does not have one rotation with three skins — she has three answers to three situations. Wendigo when you want to be in the fight, the bulky form when you are being punished, Storm Hawk when the ground is a problem or the pack is spread out. Choosing the right shape for the moment is the skill the class is built to test.

## A Starting Shape, Not a Finished Build

Community Season 9 notes describe opening with **Thorned Roots** — a root-and-terrain spell — as the class's early rhythm. That is a starting point, not a Season 10 verdict, so treat it as the shape to test first:

1. **Campaign to 100 on the summoner core.** Let the Carrion Worm and Ravens hold attention while you learn the root puzzle. The [beginner guide](/guides/hero-siege-beginner-guide/) covers the leveling mistakes every class shares.
2. **Test each form in the same content.** Run identical packs as Wendigo and as the bulky form and Storm Hawk — the differences decide which form you commit [Ether Tree](/guides/hero-siege-ether-tree-guide/) nodes to.
3. **Commit at Hell, not before.** The [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) is where the clear nerf matters most; that is where you find out which trees carry you.
4. **Enter endgame through the [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) loop** the way any new class does, funding gear through the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) rather than through a fixed gear list.

## Why This Page Is Shorter Than the Others

Every other class on this site can lean on a settled body of community writing. The Prophet's is a season old and contradicted by a patch. Publishing a fake-complete skill breakdown would be easy and wrong — a single invented node name in a class with no prior is how a wiki loses trust. When the season's tuning settles, this page grows into the full dissection; until then it is an anatomy, a warning, and a starting shape.

## Prophet FAQ

### Should I make the Prophet my first character of the season?

Not unless you specifically want the newest kit. She asks you to solve a class rather than execute a known one, and the season's free picks or the [Viking](/guides/hero-siege-viking-build/) standard are better teachers for a first character.

### Is Storm Hawk's flight useful for farming?

It changes map traversal, which is why it matters beyond combat — but whether that translates into a farming speed edge is exactly the kind of question Season 10's documentation has not settled. Test it yourself rather than trusting a season-old clip.

### Can the Prophet carry a group?

The summoner core and the bulky form both suit a team role, and a class that can fly over terrain is a natural body for mechanics that punish grounded players. Community grouping impressions are still thin; the honest answer is that her team value is untested this season.
