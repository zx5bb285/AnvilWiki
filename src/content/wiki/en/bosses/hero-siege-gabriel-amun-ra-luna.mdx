## The Middle Layer of the Uber Roster

Hero Siege's [Uber roster](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) reads like strata. The [classic trio](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/) — Damien, Reaper, Sung Lee — anchors the bottom. [Season 10's three new Ubers](/bosses/hero-siege-phantom-leviathan/) occupy the top. And between them sits the modern layer: **Gabriel, Amun Ra and Luna** — the fights that defined the game's mid-era endgame and still anchor farming rotations today.

This page covers the layer as a whole; each fight gets its own section below. Fight-specific patterns shift as patches tune them, so the focus is what stays true: where the fights sit, what they demand, and how they farm.

<Callout type="warn" title="Patterns documented by the community">
Hero Siege's official documentation does not cover Uber mechanics in detail. What follows reflects community practice in Season 10; treat exact patterns and numbers as current-patch knowledge, not permanent facts.
</Callout>

## What the Modern Layer Expects

The modern Ubers are the game's first serious gear check above the [classic trio](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/). Concretely, the layer assumes:

- **A completed build core.** Campaign momentum clears the classic layer; the modern layer starts punishing builds with unresolved [damage plans](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/).
- **Endgame-tier survivability.** The fights' burst windows punish paper defenses — the [durability habits](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) built through the [difficulty ladder](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) stop being optional here.
- **Movement discipline.** Across all three fights, the community's advice converges on the same skill: repositioning during transition windows instead of greedy damage.

## Gabriel

Gabriel is the modern layer's opener in most farming rotations. The fight's community reputation: a check on sustained damage uptime — windows to deal damage are generous, but standing in the wrong place during transitions erases the advantage. Builds that clear [classic Ubers](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/) without a plan tend to hit their first wall here and leave with a shopping list: more sustain, more [movement quality](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/), fewer greedy habits.

## Amun Ra

Amun Ra is the layer's durability exam. Where Gabriel checks damage uptime, this fight checks whether your build can survive contact — sustained pressure that punishes paper-thin defense far more than slow damage. The farming consequence is real: builds that skip the durability band of their [BiS roadmap](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) feel this fight twice as hard as their gear score suggests.

## Luna

Luna is the layer's speed check and, for many builds, its main farming draw. The fight rewards fast, clean execution, and it has a dedicated place in the endgame economy — which is why dedicated Luna farming guides exist as their own genre. A Luna rotation slot pairs naturally with [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pushes in a farming week.

## The Modern Layer in a Season 10 Rotation

A standard endgame week runs all three layers: [classic trio](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/) for volume, the modern layer for mid-tier checks and Luna farming, and [Season 10's new Ubers](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) for the top of the loot ladder. The modern layer's role is the hinge — hard enough to test the build, routine enough to farm weekly.

## Modern Uber FAQ

### What level should I be for the modern Ubers?

Level 100 with a completed [endgame build core](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) is the practical entry — the fights check gear quality more than raw level.

### Which of the three should I farm first?

Gabriel for the progression check, Luna for farming value once the build is stable. Amun Ra tends to enter rotations as builds complete their durability slots.

### Do modern Ubers drop Season 10's new items?

The [S10 loot additions](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) distribute across the endgame, and the modern layer remains a standard source of high-tier drops. Exact per-boss loot tables are community-documented — verify in-game as patches continue.
