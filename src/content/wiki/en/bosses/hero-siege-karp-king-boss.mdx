## The Guardian of the Karp Kingdom

Act 5 hands you a region with its own identity — the **Land of the Karp Kingdom** — and then a boss who represents it. **Karp King** is what this wiki would call a zone guardian: a fight whose purpose is to confirm you belong in the part of the campaign you have reached, not to spring a new lesson on you.

That framing is useful for how you approach him. Acts 3 and 4 already taught you to build a plan and to execute it. Karp King is where the game checks that the plan *holds up over a long fight* — and rewards players who can keep output steady rather than spiking once and coasting.

<Callout type="info" title="A midpoint fight, not a midpoint rest">
Do not read "zone guardian" as "pushover." Act 5 is the boundary between the campaign's teaching half and its survival half. Karp King is the first boss who expects you to keep a rhythm for minutes, not seconds.
</Callout>

## The Rhythm of the Land of the Karp Kingdom

The region sets the tone: open stretches, waves of enemies, and a fight where space is plentiful but safe ground is not constant. Karp King's pressure comes from that arena shape rather than from a single instant-kill mechanic.

Practically, the fight rewards three habits:

- **Steady damage uptime.** Anchor your output to the recoveries between his area patterns, and keep it consistent. A build that only functions in short bursts will find Act 5's boss fights feel twice as long as they are.
- **Repositioning that does not cost you your rotation.** The Kingdom's arenas give you room; the test is using it without going dark on damage. This is the muscle Acts 7 and 8 will lean on hardest.
- **Reading the arena, not the boss.** Much of what punishes you here is where you are standing relative to the pattern, not the pattern itself. That reading skill is the through-line of the [Act bosses ladder](/bosses/hero-siege-act-bosses-guide/).

If Karp King feels like a slog rather than a wall, that is the intended experience — it is calibrating you for the endurance fights ahead, and it is a good moment to audit whether your [build](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) has the sustain to match its burst.

## Karp King's Bellybutton and the Recipe

Unusual name, load-bearing function: **Karp King's Bellybutton** is the Act 5 ingredient of the **Key of Terror**. The full recipe is one piece per act boss:

**Gurag's Soul (Act 1) + Death's Sigil (Act 2) + Anubis' Ankh (Act 3) + Damien's Eye (Act 4) + Karp King's Bellybutton (Act 5) + Satan's Horn (Act 6).**

By the time you reach Act 5 you are halfway through that list, which is the useful way to think about it: the campaign and the key recipe advance together. You are not making a separate farming trip for the Bellybutton — you are collecting it as you clear. The recipe itself and its payoff fight live on the [Fallen Damien page](/bosses/hero-siege-damien-boss/).

## Why the Mid-Campaign Bosses Matter More Than They Feel Like

It is tempting to treat Acts 5 and 6 as connective tissue between the Act 3 build lesson and the Act 7 endurance test. That undersells them. Karp King is where a build's *sustainability* is first measured: whether you can hold a rotation through a long health bar and shifting floor danger, not just deliver a burst window. Builds that pass him on Normal often develop their first real cracks on Nightmare, because the fight's demands scale with the same rhythm rather than with a new mechanic.

## Karp King FAQ

### Do I need to beat Karp King to finish the campaign?

Yes — he is Act 5's gate and the campaign is linear, so the kill is required to progress on every difficulty, not optional content.

### Is the Bellybutton a rare drop?

It is the Act boss's recipe piece, so it enters your Key of Terror chain as you clear the act. Treat the exact drop behavior as patch-sensitive and confirm in-game; the important part is that Act 5 is the ingredient's source, which is stable.

### Which build types struggle most here?

Burst-only builds that run out of resources between big hits. Karp King's length exposes them, because the fight is won with sustained output rather than a single window. If that describes your character, the [difficulty guide](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/)'s notes on scaling will matter more to you than they did in Acts 1 through 4.
