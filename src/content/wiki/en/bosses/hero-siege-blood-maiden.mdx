## The Third Name of the S10 Trio

[Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) closed its Uber roster with three names: [Phantom Leviathan](/bosses/hero-siege-phantom-leviathan/), [Captain Grimtide](/bosses/hero-siege-captain-grimtide/), and **Blood Maiden** — the layer that now defines the top of Hero Siege's [endgame](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/). Launched with Ebontharn on August 21, 2026, the trio took over from the [modern layer](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/) as the game's hardest content and its most valuable loot source in the same patch.

## Why the New Layer Absorbed Into Rotations Immediately

Usually a new boss takes weeks to enter farming routines — strategies need writing, builds need adjusting. The S10 trio skipped that lag, for one reason: **loot gravity**. The [peak-tier items](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) endgame builds chase and the [season's new uniques](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) concentrate at the top of the [Uber ladder](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/), so every serious build had a farming reason to learn the fights in week one.

That dynamic shapes everything practical about Blood Maiden: the fight is both the exam and the reward, and the farming rotation is the study plan.

## Earning the Kill: the Progression That Gets You There

The path to a routine Blood Maiden kill runs through the rest of the roster — each layer is the training ground for the next:

1. **Finish the build on the [classic trio](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/).** Volume farming, easy kills, gear income — the layer that pays for everything above it.
2. **Prove the build on the [modern layer](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/).** Gabriel, Amun Ra and Luna are the readiness test: clean kills there mean the [Satanic core](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) and [durability band](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) are real.
3. **Learn the new layer with the [season's builds](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) at full power.** Peak-tier slots first, current footage always — the embedded video covers all three S10 Ubers.

## The Farming Math

For an endgame build, a weekly [Uber rotation](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) in Season 10 reads like a portfolio: the [classic](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/) layer is the steady income, the [modern layer](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/) is the mid-band value, and the S10 trio is the high-variance upside — the kills most likely to produce a [season-defining drop](/items/hero-siege-unholy-items/).

The honest caveat: upside concentrates where difficulty does. Until your kill times on the new layer are routine, the [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) grind and [market income](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) remain the reliable part of the [farming week](/guides/hero-siege-gold-farming-guide/) — and there is no shame in farming below your ambition while the build catches up.

## Blood Maiden FAQ

### Which of the three new Ubers should I learn first?

Whichever your build kills fastest — and the honest answer is usually decided by trying. The three fights form one layer; farming rotations treat them as a set, not as a sequence.

### Does Blood Maiden need a specific class or build?

No — the new layer is class-agnostic as a requirement, even if kill times vary widely across the [tier list](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/). Preparation quality matters more than class choice.

### When will a full written strategy for this fight appear here?

When the encounter's documentation stabilizes — the plan is a phase-by-phase refresh of this page once 7.0.x tuning slows down and the community's consensus patterns hold across patches.
