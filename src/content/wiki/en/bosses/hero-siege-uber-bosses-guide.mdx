## What Uber Bosses Are

Uber bosses are Hero Siege's superboss tier: encounters above and beyond the act bosses, unlocked in the endgame and tuned to check everything your build has — sustain, burst windows, movement, and your [Incarnation Tree](/guides/hero-siege-incarnation-tree-guide/) depth. They are also among the game's best loot sources, which is why the community measures a season's progress in Uber kills.

<Callout type="warn" title="Fight specifics are still forming">
Season 10's three new Ubers launched August 21, 2026, and the community's understanding of their patterns is being documented fight by fight — early 7.0.x hotfixes also tuned encounters. Treat specific patterns you read anywhere as current-patch knowledge, not permanent facts.
</Callout>

## The Uber Roster

**Season 10's three new Ubers (Ebontharn).** The season's headline combat addition. JuxtAPoser's launch-week video "3 New Uber Bosses" is the community's primary first-look documentation, and endgame showcases (such as imortilize's Inferno Uber Luna clear) demonstrate how the new tier interacts with reworked classes. Their mechanics lean on the game's established Uber grammar — phases, burst windows, and punishing area denial — at a higher tune.

**The Gabriel / Amun Ra / Luna tier.** Introduced in the seasons around S5's Amun Ra addition, these fights are the "modern classic" Ubers: multi-phase encounters with distinct mechanics each. Amaggixz's Bard showcase running all three at H5 difficulty remains a good visual reference for their flow.

**The classic trio: Damien, Reaper, Sung Lee.** The original Uber lineup from earlier seasons, still in the game as the entry point into Uber-tier content. Benderdin's dedicated guide video covers their patterns, and they remain the recommended first Uber kills of any season — including your first character's.

## Preparation: What Every Uber Attempt Needs

1. **A complete build, not a leveling build.** Uber checks assume your [league starter](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) has already converted into its endgame form — core uniques in place, talents finished, tree spine claimed.
2. **Sustain above your pride.** Nearly every Uber death trace shows the same thing: damage output was fine, survivability wasn't. Defensive [talent](/guides/hero-siege-class-tier-list/) and Incarnation nodes are not optional at this tier.
3. **Consumables and retries budgeted.** Ubers are attempt-learning fights. Expect to die learning phase patterns; the community's advice is to bring enough supplies to lose informatively several times.
4. **Difficulty honesty.** Uber content lives at Inferno. If the current difficulty's act bosses are pushing you, [wormhole depth](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) — not Uber attempts — is your next progression step.

## Fight Fundamentals: the Shared Grammar

Across the roster, Uber fights reward the same four skills:

- **Burst windows.** Every Uber has vulnerable windows — usually after a phase transition or a whiffed big attack. Damaging output outside these windows is often wasted; learning when they occur matters more than raw DPS.
- **Area denial respect.** Ground effects and zone attacks in Uber fights punish greed. Leave the red early; re-enter when it clears.
- **Add triage.** Most Ubers spawn minions mid-fight. Killing adds too slowly loses the fight; ignoring adds entirely also loses the fight — learn which adds are urgent for your class.
- **Phase memory.** Ubers are pattern tests. The community's clearing videos are worth watching specifically for phase order, even if you never copy anyone's build.

## Which Uber Should You Kill First?

The community's standard advice: **the classic trio first, the new three when your build is proven.** The older fights teach the Uber grammar with older, better-documented patterns; the Season 10 Ubers are the exam. Once your first Uber kill funds a real upgrade — or drops something from the [unique pool](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) worth selling on the [market](/guides/hero-siege-market-guide/) — the whole endgame loop accelerates.

## FAQ

**How many Uber bosses are in Hero Siege now?**
With Season 10's three additions, the Uber roster spans the classic trio, the Gabriel/Amun Ra/Luna tier, and the new Ebontharn encounters — roughly a dozen top-end fights depending on how seasonal variants are counted.

**Can you solo Uber bosses?**
Yes — solo Uber kills are the community's standard showcase format. Co-op works too, with scaled encounter difficulty.

**Do Uber kills drop guaranteed uniques?**
Uber fights are top-tier loot sources, but drop specifics vary by fight, difficulty, and patch. Verify current reward tables in-game before farming a specific Uber for a specific item.

**My build clears wormholes but dies to Ubers. Why?**
Wormholes test sustained clearing; Ubers test burst windows and phase execution. They are different exams — see the preparation list above for the usual gaps.
