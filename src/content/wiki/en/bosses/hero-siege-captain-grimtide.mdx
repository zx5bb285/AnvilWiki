## A New Name at the Top of the Roster

[Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) added three names to Hero Siege's [Uber roster](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/), and Captain Grimtide is one of them — a superboss sitting above the [classic trio](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/) and the [modern layer](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/), where the season's hardest fights and best loot live.

Season 10 launched on August 21, 2026, which means the community's understanding of this fight is measured in weeks, not seasons. Written pattern guides are still catching up to the patch cadence — so this page does the part written guides can do honestly: the preparation you control.

## The Pre-Fight Checklist

Four gates, in order. If you pass all four, the fight is yours to learn; if any one fails, that is where the work is.

1. **Build completion.** The new Ubers punish transition-phase builds. Every core slot should carry [endgame-tier](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) gear, with the damage plan fully online — [league starter](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/) builds graduate here by design.
2. **Survivability above the modern layer.** The fights below are the calibration: if [Gabriel, Amun Ra and Luna](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/) kills are clean and boring, your [durability slots](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) are ready for the next exam. If not, the fix is there, not here.
3. **Peak-tier slots where they exist.** A [peak-tier piece](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) in the weapon or core scaling slot changes the fight's difficulty more than any strategy note — that is what the tier is for.
4. **Current-patch knowledge.** Watch a kill from the last two weeks, not a launch-week guide. The embedded video below covers all three new Ubers including Grimtide.

## What the Fight Adds to Your Rotation

The farming logic for the new layer settled fast, because loot value does not wait for documentation: top-layer Ubers concentrate the season's best drops, and [peak-tier chase pieces](/items/hero-siege-unholy-items/) plus the [S10 unique additions](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) are the whole reason endgame builds exist.

The standard endgame week in Season 10 runs all three [Uber layers](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/): [classic](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/) for volume, modern for mid-tier value, and the S10 trio for the top of the ladder — with [wormhole](/guides/hero-siege-wormhole-guide/) pushes filling the gaps. Captain Grimtide's slot in that week is whatever your kill time makes it: a boss run for slower builds, a rotation staple for faster ones.
