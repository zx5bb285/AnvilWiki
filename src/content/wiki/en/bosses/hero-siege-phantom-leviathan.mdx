## Season 10's New Ceiling

Every Hero Siege season reshuffles the top of the power curve, and [Season 10](/guides/hero-siege-season-10-guide/) did it with three new names: **Phantom Leviathan**, [Captain Grimtide](/bosses/hero-siege-captain-grimtide/) and [Blood Maiden](/bosses/hero-siege-blood-maiden/). Launched with Ebontharn on August 21, 2026, the trio now forms the top layer of the [Uber roster](/bosses/hero-siege-uber-bosses-guide/) — the fights endgame builds are measured against.

## What the Community Has Documented So Far

An honest snapshot, a little over a month into the season:

- **Roster position is settled.** Phantom Leviathan is one of the three new Uber encounters, above the [classic trio](/bosses/hero-siege-classic-uber-bosses/) and the [modern layer](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/) in the endgame progression.
- **The fight checks completed builds.** Early kill reports consistently describe it as a full-kit exam — damage uptime, survivability and movement all at once, in the pattern of every top-layer Uber before it.
- **Kill footage outpaces written guides.** The fastest way to see the fight's actual patterns is video; written documentation lags the patch cadence.

## Preparing a Build for the Fight

Whatever the specific patterns turn out to be, the preparation checklist for a top-layer Uber is stable — it is the same exam every season, harder:

1. **A completed [endgame build](/guides/hero-siege-league-starter-builds/).** The new Ubers punish half-finished transitions. If your slots are still mixed [Heroic](/items/hero-siege-heroic-items/), the [Satanic core](/items/hero-siege-satanic-items/) comes first.
2. **Top-of-ladder survivability.** Burst that erases under-geared builds is the tier's signature. The [durability discipline](/items/hero-siege-viking-bis/) built through [Hell and Inferno](/guides/hero-siege-difficulty-guide/) is the entry fee.
3. **Practice on the layers below.** Routine kills on the [modern trio](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/) are the community's standard readiness test — if those fights are clean, the new layer's mechanics are the only unknown.
4. **Loot value runs both ways.** The new layer is also the season's best loot source — [peak-tier pieces](/items/hero-siege-angelic-items/) and the [S10 uniques](/items/hero-siege-uniques-overview/) concentrate here, which is why farming rotations absorbed the trio immediately.

## Phantom Leviathan FAQ

### Can I skip the older Uber layers and go straight to Phantom Leviathan?

The Uber progression gates the new layer behind the lower ones by design — and skipping the practice is a trap anyway. The [modern layer](/bosses/hero-siege-gabriel-amun-ra-luna/) kills are the readiness check.

### What loot is Phantom Leviathan known for?

As a top-layer Uber, its drops sit at the peak of the season's item pool — the tiers endgame builds chase. Per-boss loot tables are community-documented and patch-sensitive; verify in-game.

### Why does this guide hedge so much?

Because the fight is a few weeks old and the patch cadence is active. Honest hedging now beats confident wrong answers that waste your build's resources — no invented numbers, only what the community has actually documented.
